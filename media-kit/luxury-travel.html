Module 03B · Editorial Sections

The recurring LIKE® sections

Travel is one axis of a broader editorial world. Across editions, LIKE® returns to a consistent set of sections — the same standards of fact-checking and craft applied to each — giving partners more than one credible place to live.

LIKE® Technology

Considered coverage of the tools, platforms, and ideas shaping how the elite live and work.

LIKE® Law

Policy, jurisprudence, and the questions of governance and rights that frame public life.

LIKE® Travel & Tourism

Long-form destination narratives anchored in verifiable, on-the-record places — the vertical featured throughout this kit.

LIKE® Music

Artists, chart detail, and cultural moments, reported with the same discipline as the magazine’s features.

LIKE® Entertainment

Film, stage, and the personalities of the cultural industries — storytelling over spectacle.

LIKE® Leaders

Profiles of the founders, principals, and figures at the head of business and culture.

LIKE® Diplomacy

Editor’s-view perspectives on international relations, summitry, and the work of statecraft.

Sections recur across editions rather than appearing in every issue; a partnership can be placed in the section that best fits the brand’s world.
