TRADE SHOWS · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S TRADE SHOWS

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Trade Shows · October 2026

Before there were supply chains there were fairs, and the fair is still where the physical economy introduces itself: the new machine tool, the new aircraft order, the new vintage, the new phone. This guide maps the world's great trade fairs sector by sector — 42 fairs in 11 countries.

For each fair: where it sits and when it next opens (as the organiser publishes — never projected), the organising body and its leadership, verified name by name against 2025–26 sources, in a season of successions: the CTA, the GSMA and the HKTDC all changed chief executives inside eighteen months. Each entry states the fair's scale where — and only where — the organiser publishes it, with the counting method named where it matters, and how a company exhibits or a trade visitor attends. Four fairs publish no audited scale usable to this standard, and their entries say so.

TECHNOLOGY & INDUSTRY

1. CES — LAS VEGAS, NEVADA, UNITED STATES

Founded 1967 · Consumer and enterprise technology

Calendar / next edition: Annual, January. Next edition dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: Consumer Technology Association (CTA) — US trade association; owner and producer of CES

Organizer leadership: Kinsey Fabrizio, President and CEO of CTA (effective 1 May 2026); Gary Shapiro, Executive Chair of CTA (in that role since 1 January 2026, previously CEO). Per CTA leadership-succession press release, 10 February 2026 (cta.tech). Note: CTA releases of January 2026 still styled Shapiro 'Executive Chair and CEO' during the transition.

Venue: Las Vegas Convention Center, The Venetian Expo and venues across Las Vegas

Scale (as published): CES 2026 (independently audited, published by CTA): 148,392 participants; 4,100+ exhibitors across 2.6 million+ net square feet, including some 1,200 startups in Eureka Park; 7,037 media, content creators and industry analysts; 55,841 international participants from 141 countries, regions and territories.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: consumer-electronics, automotive-tech, digital-health, robotics, AI and startup companies (Eureka Park). Attendees: industry executives, retailers, investors, policymakers and media; 52% of CES 2026 attendees were senior-level executives (CTA audit).

Media / press: Large accredited media operation: 7,037 media, content creators and analysts at CES 2026; pre-show Media Days are restricted to accredited media.

Distinctiveness: The largest single annual technology gathering in the United States and the launch platform of record for consumer technology; unusual in publishing an independently audited attendance figure.

Access / entry: Trade-only: attendance is restricted to people affiliated with the consumer technology industry, media and exhibitors, with credential verification; no general-public admission. Companies exhibit through CTA's exhibit-sales programme or Eureka Park startup packages.

Website: ces.tech · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Leadership succession 2026 is the key change versus earlier editions of this guide: Fabrizio (CTA president since before the transition) became President and CEO on 1 May 2026 and Shapiro moved to Executive Chair. CES 2025 comparative audited attendance was 142,465.

2. MOBILE WORLD CONGRESS (MWC BARCELONA) — BARCELONA, SPAIN

Founded 1987 (as the GSM World Congress; held in Barcelona since 2006) · Mobile communications and connectivity

Calendar / next edition: Annual, late February / early March. Next edition dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: GSMA — global mobile-industry association (GSMA Ltd. stages the MWC events)

Organizer leadership: Vivek Badrinath, Director General of the GSMA and member of the GSMA Board (from 1 April 2025, succeeding Mats Granryd, who served until 31 March 2025 and continued as Special Advisor to the Board for the remainder of 2025); Gopal Vittal, Chair of the GSMA Board (from 24 March 2025); John Hoffman, CEO and Director, GSMA Ltd. Per GSMA Annual Report 2025 and GSMA appointment announcements (Sept 2024).

Venue: Fira de Barcelona Gran Via

Scale (as published): GSMA-reported for MWC Barcelona 2025: approximately 109,000 attendees from 205 countries and territories and some 2,900 exhibitors, sponsors and partners (figures as reported in trade press from GSMA releases; GSMA publishes no independently audited attendance release — see research notes).

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: mobile operators, network-equipment and device makers, software and AI companies, national pavilions. Attendees: operator and vendor executives, regulators and ministers, media and analysts.

Media / press: Accredited media programme with a dedicated media centre; ministerial and policy programme runs alongside for government and regulatory delegations.

Distinctiveness: The mobile industry's global congress-exhibition: the one week when operators, vendors and communications ministers are in the same halls; the GSMA Ministerial Programme has no real equivalent at other technology shows.

Access / entry: Trade and industry only, by paid pass tiers (exhibition through to VIP); exhibiting is sold through GSMA Ltd.'s sales programme and national-pavilion organisers. No general-public admission.

Website: mwcbarcelona.com · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Director General succession verified: Granryd announced his departure in June 2024; Badrinath's appointment was announced in September 2024 and took effect 1 April 2025, immediately after MWC Barcelona 2025. The Board chair also changed in the same window (Jose Maria Alvarez-Pallete to Gopal Vittal, February–March 2025).

3. HANNOVER MESSE — HANOVER, GERMANY

Founded 1947 · Industrial technology

Calendar / next edition: Annual, April. Next edition dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: Deutsche Messe AG — German trade-fair company; shareholders are the State of Lower Saxony and the City of Hanover

Organizer leadership: Dr Jochen Kockler, Chairman of the Managing Board of Deutsche Messe AG (board member since 2012, chairman since 1 July 2017). Per Deutsche Messe corporate biography (messe.de) and Hannover Messe 2026 press releases, in which he is quoted in that title.

Venue: Hannover Exhibition Grounds (Messegelande Hannover)

Scale (as published): Deutsche Messe preview release for the 2026 edition: around 4,000 exhibiting companies from the mechanical-engineering, electrical, digital and energy industries expected. A final audited exhibitor/visitor figure for the most recent edition was not published in the sources consulted — see research notes.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: automation, drive, energy, industrial-software and subcontracting companies, plus a partner-country presentation. Attendees: manufacturing executives, engineers, public-sector and research visitors.

Media / press: Accredited press centre on the grounds; extensive conference and forum programme open to accredited media.

Distinctiveness: The world's flagship industrial fair since 1947, built around a rotating partner country and a Chancellor-level opening; the reference point against which all other industrial fairs are measured.

Access / entry: Trade-visitor fair: tickets are sold to professional visitors with industry affiliation; exhibiting is booked through Deutsche Messe's sales organisation and its foreign representatives.

Website: hannovermesse.de · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Kockler previously ran the DLG exhibitions department and built Agritechnica at the same Hanover grounds — a biographical point Deutsche Messe itself publishes. No leadership change at Deutsche Messe was found in 2024–26 sources.

4. BAUMA — MUNICH, GERMANY

Founded 1954 · Construction and mining machinery

Calendar / next edition: Triennial, April. Next edition officially published: 3–9 April 2028, Munich.

Organizer: Messe Munchen GmbH — trade-fair company owned by the Free State of Bavaria, the City of Munich and the Munich chambers of commerce and crafts

Organizer leadership: Messe Munchen dual chief executives: Dr Reinhard Pfeiffer and Stefan Rummel, CEOs (reconfirmed for a further five years by the shareholders, announced 16 May 2024). Rummel is the CEO quoted in bauma's own closing releases. Per Messe Munchen press releases 2024–2026 (messe-muenchen.de).

Venue: Trade Fair Center Messe Munchen

Scale (as published): bauma 2025 (Messe Munchen closing release): around 600,000 visitors from more than 200 countries and regions and 3,601 exhibitors from 57 nations. DISCREPANCY: Messe Munchen's own bauma NETWORK factsheet (Nov 2025) gives 3,548 exhibitors, 605,974 visitors and 614,000 sqm, marked 'not yet FKM certified' — see notes.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: construction-machinery, building-material-machine, mining-machine, construction-vehicle and component manufacturers (conceptual partner: VDMA). Attendees: contractors, dealers, rental companies and mining operators worldwide.

Media / press: Accredited press programme; bauma publishes exhibitor and visitor figures in its closing release and network factsheets.

Distinctiveness: By area the largest trade fair in the world: the 614,000 sqm 2025 edition filled the entire Munich grounds plus outdoor plant displays; it is the construction industry's triennial global ordering fair.

Access / entry: Open to trade visitors and, unlike most German B2B fairs, also admits the general public on paid tickets. Exhibiting is by application through Messe Munchen, with stand allocation by product sector.

Website: bauma.de · Contact: info@bauma.de (published on the Messe Munchen bauma NETWORK factsheet)

Flagged discrepancy (see scale): the April 2025 closing release's rounded figures (3,601 exhibitors; around 600,000 visitors) differ from the November 2025 factsheet's precise but uncertified figures (3,548; 605,974). Both are Messe Munchen publications; the certified FKM figure was still pending in the factsheet consulted.

5. GITEX GLOBAL — DUBAI, UNITED ARAB EMIRATES

Founded 1981 · Technology, AI and startups

Calendar / next edition: Annual, October (to 2025). 2026 edition officially published: GITEX Scale Summit 7 December 2026, exhibition 8–11 December 2026, at Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City Dubai — a move of both venue and season.

Organizer: Dubai World Trade Centre (DWTC) — Dubai government-owned events and venue organisation

Organizer leadership: Trixie LohMirmand, Executive Vice President, Events & Exhibitions Management, DWTC — the DWTC executive who signs for GITEX and Gulfood and is quoted as 'global organiser of GITEX and Gulfood events'; Mahir Julfar, Executive Vice President, Venue Services Management, DWTC. Per DWTC announcements at GITEX Global 2025 (October 2025). DWTC's Director General was not named in the GITEX releases consulted — see research notes.

Venue: Dubai World Trade Centre and Dubai Harbour (2025); moving to Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City Dubai, from 2026

Scale (as published): GITEX Global 2025 (DWTC releases): more than 6,800 exhibitors including 2,000 startups, 1,200 investors and delegations from more than 180 countries. DWTC did not publish a total visitor figure in the releases consulted; third-party round-ups citing 180,000+ visitors are not DWTC-audited — see research notes.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: global technology enterprises, AI and data-centre companies, government digital authorities, startups (via the co-located Expand North Star at Dubai Harbour in 2025). Attendees: CIOs, investors, ministers and technology buyers.

Media / press: Accredited media programme; DWTC issues opening and closing releases with exhibitor and delegation counts.

Distinctiveness: The largest technology show outside the US/EU axis by exhibitor count, and the only mega-show that is also an instrument of state: whole governments exhibit as national digital brands. Its 2026 relocation to Expo City with a December date is the biggest calendar change in this guide.

Access / entry: Trade and industry visitors by paid registration; exhibiting is sold by DWTC's events division, with startups routed through Expand North Star packages.

Website: gitex.com · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Venue/season change verified against DWTC's own announcement made on day two of GITEX Global 2025: GITEX and Gulfood both relocate to the expanded Dubai Exhibition Centre in 2026, Gulfood continuing across both venues. Visitor-scale gap: no DWTC-published total attendance for 2025 was found.

6. ELECTRONICA — MUNICH, GERMANY

Founded 1964 · Electronics components, systems and applications

Calendar / next edition: Biennial, November (even years). Next edition dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: Messe Munchen GmbH — trade-fair company owned by the Free State of Bavaria, the City of Munich and the Munich chambers of commerce and crafts

Organizer leadership: Messe Munchen dual chief executives: Dr Reinhard Pfeiffer and Stefan Rummel, CEOs (reconfirmed for a further five years, announced 16 May 2024). Per Messe Munchen press releases 2024–2026 (messe-muenchen.de). No show-specific director was named in the sources consulted.

Venue: Trade Fair Center Messe Munchen

Scale (as published): electronica 2024 (Messe Munchen closing figures, as reported in trade press): 3,480 exhibitors from 59 countries and around 80,000 trade visitors from 100 countries. See research notes on verification tier.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: semiconductor, embedded-systems, automotive-electronics, test-and-measurement and component manufacturers and distributors. Attendees: electronics engineers, buyers and product managers.

Media / press: Accredited press programme with pre-show press conferences by major semiconductor exhibitors.

Distinctiveness: The components industry's world fair: the show where the semiconductor supply chain — makers, distributors and EMS providers — is present in one place, co-located in alternate cycles with productronica.

Access / entry: Trade visitors only, by paid ticket with professional registration. Exhibiting is booked through Messe Munchen's electronica team and foreign sales partners.

Website: electronica.de · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Scale figures rest on Messe Munchen closing-release numbers as carried by trade press; the FKM-certified figures were not separately consulted in this pass — flagged in research notes.

7. INNOTRANS — BERLIN, GERMANY

Founded 1996 · Railway and transport technology

Calendar / next edition: Biennial, September (even years); the 2026 edition was held 22–25 September 2026. 2028 dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: Messe Berlin GmbH — trade-fair company owned by the State of Berlin

Organizer leadership: Dr Mario Tobias, Chief Executive Officer of Messe Berlin (in post since September 2023; quoted as CEO throughout Messe Berlin's 2025–26 releases). No InnoTrans-specific director was named in the sources consulted. Per Messe Berlin / ITB Berlin press materials 2025–2026.

Venue: Berlin Exhibition Grounds (Messegelande Berlin), including the outdoor track display

Scale (as published): InnoTrans 2024 (Messe Berlin closing figures, as reported in railway trade press): 2,940 exhibitors from 59 countries and around 170,000 visitors from 133 countries. See research notes on verification tier.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: rail-vehicle manufacturers, signalling and infrastructure suppliers, public-transport operators and component makers. Attendees: railway operators, transport authorities and industry engineers.

Media / press: Accredited media programme; the outdoor display of full-size vehicles is a signature media draw.

Distinctiveness: The world's leading rail fair, defined by its 3,500 metres of display track on the Berlin grounds: nowhere else do complete trains debut on rails at a trade fair.

Access / entry: Trade visitors only. Exhibiting, including rail-vehicle display slots on the outdoor track, is allocated by Messe Berlin by product segment.

Website: innotrans.de · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Scale figures rest on Messe Berlin closing-release numbers as carried by railway trade press; not separately FKM-verified in this pass — flagged in research notes.

8. IMTS — INTERNATIONAL MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGY SHOW — CHICAGO, ILLINOIS, UNITED STATES

Founded 1927 · Manufacturing technology and machine tools

Calendar / next edition: Biennial, September (even years); the 2026 edition was held 14–19 September 2026. 2028 dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: AMT — The Association For Manufacturing Technology (US trade association); AMT owns and produces IMTS

Organizer leadership: Douglas K. Woods, President of AMT (quoted in that title in AMT's IMTS 2026 opening and closing releases, September 2026). Per imts.com press releases and TSNN's IMTS 2026 report.

Venue: McCormick Place, Chicago

Scale (as published): IMTS 2026 (AMT-published): 91,295 registrants, including 14,447 students, from all 50 states and 123 countries; 1,788 exhibiting companies (400 of them first-time) across 1.17 million sq. ft. of exhibit space.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: machine-tool, automation, additive-manufacturing, software and metrology companies. Attendees: job-shop owners, production engineers, OEM manufacturing executives, investors and students.

Media / press: Accredited media programme with an on-site media centre; AMT publishes registrant (not turnstile) figures — see notes.

Distinctiveness: The largest manufacturing-technology show in the Western Hemisphere, filling all four McCormick Place buildings; the biennial ordering cycle of the US metalworking industry turns on it.

Access / entry: Trade and industry registration (paid), with a large student programme; not a public show. Exhibiting is sold by AMT with space assigned by product pavilion.

Website: imts.com · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Terminology flag: AMT reports 'registrants' (91,295 for 2026), not audited attendance; the figure includes 14,447 students. Comparisons with turnstile-counted shows should note this.

9. LOGIMAT — STUTTGART, GERMANY

Founded 2003 · Intralogistics solutions and process management

Calendar / next edition: Annual, March; the 2026 edition was held 24–26 March 2026. In September 2026 EUROEXPO and Messe Stuttgart extended the Stuttgart venue partnership to at least 2031; the 2027 edition (25th anniversary) follows in March 2027, exact dates as published by the organiser.

Organizer: EUROEXPO Messe- und Kongress-GmbH (Munich) — private trade-fair organiser; venue partner Messe Stuttgart

Organizer leadership: Christoph Huss, Managing Director of EUROEXPO Messe- und Kongress-GmbH; Michael Ruchty, Exhibition Director of LogiMAT (EUROEXPO). Venue side: Stefan Lohnert, Managing Director (styled President in some English-language releases) of Messe Stuttgart. Per EUROEXPO/Messe Stuttgart joint releases, January–September 2026.

Venue: Messe Stuttgart (Stuttgart Trade Fair Centre, by Stuttgart Airport)

Scale (as published): LogiMAT 2026 (EUROEXPO pre-show release): more than 1,600 exhibitors from more than 40 countries, all ten halls and over 120,000 sqm of exhibition space, fully booked. A final visitor figure was not published in the sources consulted — see research notes.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: warehouse-automation, robotics, materials-handling, software and packaging companies. Attendees: logistics, production and e-commerce operations managers from industry, retail and services.

Media / press: Accredited press programme; the organiser publishes exhibitor and space figures pre-show.

Distinctiveness: Europe's annual intralogistics marketplace, grown from a 2003 regional show into a sell-out of the entire Stuttgart grounds; its September 2026 renewal to 2031 locks Stuttgart in as its home against rival German venues.

Access / entry: Trade visitors only, by paid ticket. Exhibiting is sold by EUROEXPO; the show is fully booked well ahead, with waiting-list allocation.

Website: logimat-messe.de · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Title variance flagged: Stefan Lohnert is 'Managing Director' in Messe Stuttgart's own German-derived releases and 'President' in English trade coverage of the same releases; the German title is used here. LogiMAT spin-offs (China, India) are separate editions and are not counted in the Stuttgart scale.

THE GREAT GENERAL FAIRS & CONSUMER SHOWS

10. CANTON FAIR (CHINA IMPORT AND EXPORT FAIR) — GUANGZHOU, GUANGDONG, CHINA

Founded 1957 · General import and export fair (all consumer and industrial goods)

Calendar / next edition: Twice yearly, in three phases each session: spring session mid-April to early May; autumn session mid-October to early November. The 139th session ran 15 April–5 May 2026; the 140th (autumn) session follows the same three-phase pattern from mid-October 2026.

Organizer: China Foreign Trade Centre (CFTC) — state-owned enterprise group under China's Ministry of Commerce; the Fair is co-hosted by the Ministry of Commerce and the People's Government of Guangdong Province

Organizer leadership: Zhu Yong, Director General of the China Foreign Trade Centre (quoted in that title at the 139th Fair's April 2026 press conferences). Per Xinhua/Ministry of Commerce reporting, April 2026.

Venue: China Import and Export Fair Complex (Canton Fair Complex), Pazhou, Guangzhou

Scale (as published): 139th Fair, spring 2026 (Ministry of Commerce, pre-opening): 1.55 million sqm of exhibition space, 75,700 booths and more than 32,000 participating enterprises — all stated to be record highs; the first two phases alone drew 245,000 overseas buyers from 219 countries and regions (CFTC figure, 29 April 2026).

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: Chinese manufacturers and trading companies across 179 exhibition zones (139th session), plus an international pavilion of foreign exhibitors. Attendees: overseas buyers, sourcing agents and retail chains (Walmart, Tesco and Carrefour among the 290 leading purchasing enterprises confirmed for the 139th session).

Media / press: State-organised press operation; figures are issued through Ministry of Commerce press conferences and Xinhua rather than audited releases — see notes.

Distinctiveness: China's oldest and largest trade fair, running unbroken since 1957 and still the single largest sourcing event on earth by booth count; officially styled the 'barometer of China's foreign trade'.

Access / entry: Trade only: overseas buyers register for a buyer badge with business credentials; Chinese exhibitors are allocated booths through provincial trading delegations, with a separate application route for the international pavilion. A permanent online platform runs between sessions.

Website: cantonfair.org.cn · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Counting-method note: Canton Fair 'participating enterprises' and booth counts are not comparable with Western exhibitor counts, and buyer figures are cumulative phase counts published by the organiser/state media, not independently audited figures.

11. IFA BERLIN — BERLIN, GERMANY

Founded 1924 · Consumer electronics and home appliances

Calendar / next edition: Annual, early September; IFA 2026 was held 4–8 September 2026. 2027 dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry (the organiser's site is already badged 'IFA Berlin 2027').

Organizer: IFA Management GmbH — joint venture of gfu Consumer & Home Electronics GmbH (brand owner) and Clarion Events; staged on Messe Berlin's grounds. NOTE: on 6 October 2026 Informa announced the acquisition of Clarion Events (expected to complete Q4 2026), which will change the JV's ownership — see notes.

Organizer leadership: Leif Lindner (also rendered Leif-Erik Lindner), Chief Executive Officer of IFA Management GmbH (in post since 2023; quoted as CEO in IFA's 2025–26 releases); Carine Chardon, Managing Director of gfu Consumer & Home Electronics GmbH. Per IFA Berlin press releases (ifa-berlin.com), November 2025–September 2026.

Venue: Berlin Exhibition Grounds (Messe Berlin), including the Sommergarten

Scale (as published): IFA 2025 (official IFA closing release, 19 Nov 2025): over 1,900 exhibitors from 49 countries and around 220,000 visitors from 140 countries. IFA 2026 (organiser figures reported by TSNN): 240,000 visitors from 155 countries and more than 2,000 exhibitors.

Who exhibits & attends: Exhibitors: consumer-electronics, home-appliance, computing, gaming, beauty-tech and mobility brands. Attendees: retail buyers and chains, media and content creators, and the general public.

Media / press: One of the largest media operations of any trade show: two pre-show Media Days for accredited journalists precede the public opening, with global brand press conferences.

Distinctiveness: A century old (1924) and the world's largest home-and-consumer-tech show by public attendance; uniquely mixes a retail trade fair, a public festival (Sommergarten concerts) and a global media launch week.

Access / entry: Mixed model: trade visitors and media by accreditation/paid trade tickets; the general public is admitted on paid day tickets. Exhibiting is sold by IFA Management by product hall.

Website: ifa-berlin.com · Contact: Exhibitor enquiries via the show's official website

Ownership watch: Informa's announced acquisition of Clarion Events (announced 6 October 2026, completion expected Q4 2026, Clarion CEO Lisa Hannant to join Informa's leadership) means the IFA Management JV partner changes hands; the gfu shareholding is unaffected in the announcement. Name variance: the CEO signs as 'Leif Lindner' in IFA's own 2026 material; 'Leif-Erik Lindner' appears in earlier coverage.

12. GAMESCOM — COLOGNE, GERMANY

Founded 2009 · Video games and interactive entertainment

Calendar / next edition: Annual, late August; the 2026 edition was held in August 2026 (gamescom congress 2026 ran during the show week). 2027 dates not yet published in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: Koelnmesse GmbH (trade-fair company majority-owned by the City of Cologne and the State of North Rhine-Westphalia) jointly with game — the German Games Industry Association (brand co-owner)

Organizer leadership: Koelnmesse Executive Board: Gerald Bose, President and Chief Executive Officer, and Oliver Frese (per Koelnmesse company portrait, July 2025, and gamescom 2025 imprint). Co-organiser: Felix Falk, Managing Director of game. Supervisory Board chair: Henriette Reker, Mayor of Cologne.

Venue: Koelnmesse exhibition centre, Cologne

Scale (as published): gamescom 2025 (Koelnmesse/game closing figures, as reported in trade press): 357,000 visitors and 1,568 exhibitors from 72 countries on 233,000 sqm. For 2026 the organisers announced a record 1,600+ exhibitors from 67 countries and a fully booked 233,000 sqm before opening. See research notes on verification tier.

Media / press: Accredited media and creator programme at scale: Opening Night Live is a globally streamed showcase produced with the show.

Distinctiveness: The world's largest games event by attendance and floor space, and the only mega-show that is simultaneously a B2B marketplace, a fan festival and a streamed global broadcast (Opening Night Live).

Access / entry: Mixed model: a trade-only business area (trade-visitor tickets with professional verification) and public entertainment halls on paid day tickets that routinely sell out. Exhibiting is sold by Koelnmesse; indie developers apply for curated indie-area packages.

Website: gamescom.global · Contact: info@koelnmesse.de (Koelnmesse corporate contact, published in the gamescom imprint)

The 2025 visitor figure counts public and trade attendance together over five days, unlike trade-only shows in this guide; the organisers' 2026 pre-show exhibitor record (1,600+) was announced before the show opened.

13. HONG KONG ELECTRONICS FAIR (AUTUMN EDITION) — HONG KONG, CHINA (HONG KONG SAR)

Media / press: Accredited media programme run by the HKTDC, with press conferences tied to the October tech-fair cluster.

Date correction flagged: briefing material for this guide suggested an October 2024 start for Sophia Chong. The 2025 date is used here. Scale gap: no standalone audited figure for this fair (see scale field).

MOBILITY, TRAVEL & THE AIR SHOWS

14. PARIS AIR SHOW (SALON INTERNATIONAL DE L'AERONAUTIQUE ET DE L'ESPACE) — LE BOURGET, PARIS, FRANCE

Founded 1909 · Aerospace and defence

Calendar / next edition: Biennial, June (odd years); the 55th edition was held 16–22 June 2025. The 56th edition follows in June 2027; exact dates not confirmed in the official sources consulted for this entry.

Organizer: SIAE (Salon International de l'Aeronautique et de l'Espace) — wholly owned subsidiary of GIFAS, the French Aerospace Industries Association

Organizer leadership: Guillaume Bourdeloux, Chief Executive Officer (head) of the Paris Air Show / SIAE (appointment announced by GIFAS); Guillaume Faury, President of GIFAS (and CEO of Airbus); Frederic Parisot, Chief Executive (Delegue General) of GIFAS. Per GIFAS/SIAE appointment release and GIFAS 2025 show materials.

Venue: Parc des Expositions de Paris-Le Bourget (Le Bourget Airport)

Scale (as published): 55th edition, 2025 (SIAE closing figures, as widely reported): 2,498 exhibitors from 48 countries; 292,579 visitors (127,312 professionals and 165,267 general public); 150 aircraft on display; 1,850 accredited journalists; 322 official delegations. DISCREPANCY: pre-show briefings cited '2,400 exhibitors' — the closing figure of 2,498 is used here.
