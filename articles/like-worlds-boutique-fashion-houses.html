STYLE · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S BOUTIQUE FASHION HOUSES

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Style · October 2026

Beneath the 43 majors of the companion Fashion Houses guide runs a second fashion industry: the independent houses. Some are a designer, an atelier and a cult. Some — Lemaire, Ganni, Nanushka — have become real businesses without surrendering the name over the door. This guide maps 35 of them across five continents, each with its designer, its owner (the honest part: several are no longer purely independent, and the entries say so), its flagships where they exist, and its signature.

A note on what "boutique" means here. It is a tier, not a size of shop: houses whose centre of gravity is a named designer and a point of view, selling through a handful of doors and the world's directional stockists rather than a global boutique network. Houses graduate out of it — Jacquemus, The Row, Sacai and Dries Van Noten, all named in the brief for this list, already sit in the majors guide and are not repeated here.

Private houses publish almost nothing financial. Where a figure exists (Ganni is the exception that proves the rule), it is printed; where it does not, the entry admits it. That silence is itself the fact about this tier: its currency is standing, not scale.
