FILM · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S COMIC CONVENTIONS

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Film · October 2026

The comic convention began as a fans' meeting in a hotel ballroom and became one of the entertainment industry's load-bearing structures: the rooms where studios launch franchises, publishers take the temperature of their readers, and — in Tokyo — three hundred thousand people a day buy comics from the artists who drew them. This guide maps 27 conventions in 14 countries.

For each convention: its organising body — nonprofit, commercial portfolio, or, in Comiket's case, a committee of the doujin circles themselves — and its leadership where the body publishes it; its venue and calendar; who exhibits and who attends; and its scale, stated with a discipline this field rarely gets: every figure carries its year and its basis, because a "turnstile" count, a tickets-sold count and a unique-attendee count are three different numbers wearing the same costume. Where an organiser publishes no usable figure, the entry says so.

NORTH AMERICA

1. COMIC-CON INTERNATIONAL: SAN DIEGO (SAN DIEGO COMIC-CON) — SAN DIEGO, CALIFORNIA, UNITED STATES

Founded 1970 · Pop-culture convention — comics, film, television, publishing

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 4 days plus Preview Night, in July. Next edition as officially published: July 23-26, 2026, Preview Night July 22, 2026

Organizing body: Comic-Con International (San Diego Comic Convention) — California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation, 501(c)(3) nonprofit

Organizer leadership: President of the Board of Directors: Robin Donlan, as listed in the Comic-Con 2026 Souvenir Book staff list; Chief Communications and Strategy Officer: David Glanzer

Venue: San Diego Convention Center, plus Gaslamp Quarter and off-site venues

Scale (as published, with basis): More than 135,000 attendees in 2025, as stated by the City of San Diego / San Diego Convention Center (organizer-aligned official source). Basis: capacity-capped attendance, generally reported as unique attendees; CCI itself does not publish a separate audited unique-vs-turnstile breakdown — see notes

Who exhibits & attends: Marvel Comics, DC Comics and other comics publishers, Hollywood film and television studios, streaming services, games and toy companies, Artist Alley creators, literary and academic programming; attendees are fans, professionals, retailers and press from more than 20 countries

Media / press: Press badges by application and verification through Comic-Con International; press must register in advance and meet CCI credentialing requirements as published on comic-con.org

Distinctiveness: The reference pop-culture convention worldwide; home of the Will Eisner Comic Industry Awards and the Comic-Con Museum in Balboa Park; Hall H first-look Hollywood presentations define the summer publicity calendar

Access / entry: Fans: a free Comic-Con Member ID account is required before any badge sale; badges are sold online in announced returning-registration and open-registration sales and sell out. Companies: exhibitor, Artist Alley and publisher applications through CCI's exhibitor portal during published windows

Website: https://www.comic-con.org · Contact: Via contact forms and Member ID account messaging on comic-con.org; no general public contact email is published

Attendance is capacity-limited by the Convention Center and has been reported at about 135,000 since about 2010; figures above that level in secondary sources usually add off-site activation visitors and are not CCI attendance. Long-serving president John Rogers (1986-2018) died in 2018; do not cite him as current leadership

2. NEW YORK COMIC CON (NYCC) — NEW YORK, NEW YORK, UNITED STATES

Founded 2006 · Pop-culture convention — comics, anime, film, television, gaming

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 4 days, in October, preceded by a Retailer Day. Next edition as officially published on newyorkcomiccon.com

Organizing body: ReedPop, part of RX (RELX) — commercial organizer

Organizer leadership: RX Chief Executive: Hugh Jones; ReedPop US Comics Portfolio Vice President and NYCC show lead: Kristina Rogers, as quoted in RX releases for NYCC, C2E2 and ECCC 2024-2025. ReedPop group leadership was reported by Publishers Weekly in 2023 as Mike Kisken and Simon Maxwell after Lance Fensterman's departure

Venue: Jacob K. Javits Convention Center

Scale (as published, with basis): Organizer-reported: more than 200,000 attendees in 2024 (RX, October 21, 2024). Basis AMBIGUOUS / FLAGGED: ReedPop confirmed in 2014 and again in later reporting that NYCC counts tickets sold, not unique individuals, so a 4-day buyer counts multiple times — this is a turnstile-type total, not a unique-attendee count, and is not directly comparable with San Diego's capped figure

Who exhibits & attends: Marvel, DC and other publishers, Hollywood studios and streamers (HBO, Netflix, Disney and others in 2024), anime and toy companies, more than 500 Artist Alley participants (650+ exhibitors cited by ReedPop for a later edition), retailers on Retailer Day

Media / press: Press badges by advance application through ReedPop / NYCC press registration, with editorial verification; separate creator, professional and exhibitor credentials as published on the show site

Distinctiveness: Largest pop-culture convention in North America by organizer-reported ticket total; the East Coast counterpart to San Diego, with a dedicated Retailer Day from 2024 and a large professional programme

Access / entry: Fans: badges sold online through NYCC's ticketing system after a published on-sale date, with fan verification used in recent editions; single-day and multi-day badges. Companies: exhibitor and Artist Alley applications through ReedPop's exhibitor portal

Website: https://www.newyorkcomiccon.com · Contact: Via the contact / enquiry forms on newyorkcomiccon.com and RX / ReedPop corporate enquiries on rxglobal.com

The 'bigger than San Diego' claim depends entirely on counting method: NYCC = tickets sold (turnstile-type), San Diego = capped unique attendance. LIKE must never rank the two on headline numbers without stating the basis. A 250,000+ figure was reported by ReedPop for the October 2026 edition in secondary coverage after this file's main verification pass — treat as organizer-reported tickets-sold on the same basis

3. ANIME EXPO (AX) — LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA, UNITED STATES

Founded 1992 · Anime and Japanese pop-culture convention

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 4 days, over the July 4 weekend. Next edition as officially published: Anime Expo returns to the Los Angeles Convention Center in early July 2027 (July 2-5, 2027, per SPJA's 2026 wrap announcement)

Organizing body: Society for the Promotion of Japanese Animation (SPJA) — 501(c)(3) nonprofit charitable and educational organization (status changed to 501(c)(3) in 2020)

Organizer leadership: Chief Executive Officer of SPJA: Ray Chiang, quoted as SPJA CEO in Anime Expo's own 2026 wrap release; SPJA appointed Ray Chiang COO and Mark Manansala CEO in 2016 — the CEO title in the organizer's current releases is Ray Chiang

Venue: Los Angeles Convention Center and L.A. LIVE venues

Scale (as published, with basis): Organizer-reported TURNSTILE: over 410,000 turnstile attendance in 2025 (SPJA / anime-expo.org, July 7, 2025), from more than 65 countries; 2024 was reported at about 407,000 turnstile (estimate) and 2023 at more than 392,000 turnstile. Basis is explicitly turnstile (each day's entry counted), NOT unique attendees. SPJA historically published uniques alongside turnstile (e.g. about 110,000 uniques in an earlier edition); no unique figure was published for 2025 — do not convert turnstile to uniques

Who exhibits & attends: Japanese anime studios and production companies, Aniplex, Bandai Namco, Kadokawa and other Japanese industry, US anime publishers and streamers (Crunchyroll and others), games companies, manga publishers, Artist Alley; industry, creators and fans

Media / press: Press badges by advance application through anime-expo.org with outlet verification; separate industry / professional badges as published by SPJA

Distinctiveness: North America's largest Japanese pop-culture celebration; world and North American anime premieres, Anisong concerts and a full industry marketplace; the turnstile-vs-unique gap is the textbook example of convention counting differences

Access / entry: Fans: credentials (badges) sold online from early in the year, shipped or collected on site; the show sells out. Companies: exhibitor, Artist Alley and industry registration through SPJA's published exhibitor programme

Website: https://www.anime-expo.org · Contact: Via contact forms on anime-expo.org and SPJA at spja.org

FLAGGED BASIS: every headline Anime Expo number since 2023 is turnstile. A turnstile total of 410,000 over 4 days implies roughly 100,000 people per day, not 410,000 different people. Secondary listicles that rank AX as 'larger than San Diego and New York combined' repeat the turnstile figure without the basis and must not be followed

4. MEGACON ORLANDO — ORLANDO, FLORIDA, UNITED STATES

Founded 1993 · Pop-culture convention — comics, sci-fi, horror, anime, gaming

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 4 days, in winter / spring. Editions as officially published: February 6-9, 2025; March 19-22, 2026

Organizing body: FAN EXPO HQ, part of Informa (Informa Markets / Informa Connect) — commercial organizer

Organizer leadership: President, FAN EXPO HQ: Aman Gupta, as named in FAN EXPO HQ company announcements; Vice President, FAN EXPO HQ: Andrew Moyes, quoted for FAN EXPO shows in 2024. No separate MEGACON-only show director is named in the organizer releases checked

Venue: Orange County Convention Center

Scale (as published, with basis): More than 160,000 attendees in 2025, as reported in Orlando trade / convention press (Exhibit City News, February 2025) citing the show. Basis NOT STATED by the organizer in a published release located for this file — FLAGGED as ambiguous (unique vs turnstile / tickets not specified). Pre-show material expected over 180,000, which is a forecast, not a result, and must not be used as attendance

Who exhibits & attends: Comics publishers and creators, film and television celebrities, anime voice actors, gaming, horror and cosplay programming, more than 1,000 exhibitors (2025, per trade reports), Artist Alley and tattoo / collectibles vendors in earlier editions

Media / press: Press passes by advance application through FAN EXPO HQ / MEGACON Orlando press registration, with verification as published on megaconorlando.com

Distinctiveness: The Southeast's largest fan convention and, in 2025, operator of the largest single exhibition-hall footprint of any North American fan convention (the full North/South Concourse, about 950,000 sq ft, per show reporting); noted for after-hours theme-park events at Universal Orlando

Access / entry: Fans: tickets sold online in advance (4-day, single-day, VIP / Ultimate packages), with advance pricing tiers. Companies: exhibitor, Artist Alley and sponsorship applications through FAN EXPO HQ

Website: https://fanexpohq.com/megaconorlando/ · Contact: Via contact and exhibitor enquiry forms on fanexpohq.com / megaconorlando.com; FAN EXPO HQ corporate contact via Informa

Attendance sourcing is weaker here than for RX shows: no organizer post-show release with a figure and basis was located on fanexpohq.com. The 160,000+ figure is trade-press reported; a claim of over 200,000 for 2026 in tertiary sources is unverified against an organizer release and is not used as the headline figure

5. FAN EXPO CANADA — TORONTO, ONTARIO, CANADA

Founded 1995 · Pop-culture convention — comics, sci-fi, horror, anime, gaming

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 4 days, in late August. Next edition as officially published on fanexpocanada.com

Organizing body: FAN EXPO HQ, part of Informa — commercial organizer. Founded as the Canadian National Comic Book Expo; acquired into the Hobby Star / FAN EXPO lineage and then Informa

Organizer leadership: President, FAN EXPO HQ: Aman Gupta; Vice President, FAN EXPO HQ: Andrew Moyes, quoted as FAN EXPO HQ VP in 2024 preview interviews

Venue: Metro Toronto Convention Centre

Scale (as published, with basis): Over 125,000 attendees in 2024, as reported in Toronto coverage of the August 22-25, 2024 edition; FAN EXPO's own exhibitor material lists more than 135,000 attendees and more than 1,000 exhibitors as its standing claim. Basis NOT STATED in either source — FLAGGED as ambiguous; the 135,000 figure is a standing organizer claim reused across years, not a dated audited result

Who exhibits & attends: Comics publishers and creators, film and television celebrities, anime and gaming guests, horror programming, more than 1,000 exhibitors per organizer material, Artist Alley and retailers

Media / press: Press passes by advance application through FAN EXPO Canada press registration with verification, as published on fanexpocanada.com

Distinctiveness: Canada's largest pop-culture event and, per the organizer, the third-largest in North America; the flagship of the FAN EXPO portfolio, with the Masters of Cosplay Grand Prix

Access / entry: Fans: tickets sold online in advance (multi-day, single-day, premium and VIP packages). Companies: exhibitor, Artist Alley and sponsorship applications through FAN EXPO HQ

Website: https://fanexpohq.com/fanexpocanada/ · Contact: Via contact and exhibitor enquiry forms on fanexpohq.com; Informa corporate contact for FAN EXPO HQ

Discrepancy flagged: 125,000+ (2024 dated reporting) vs 135,000 (undated standing claim in exhibitor / preview material, also used for a later edition's preview). LIKE uses the dated 2024 figure as headline and records the standing claim separately

6. EMERALD CITY COMIC CON (ECCC) — SEATTLE, WASHINGTON, UNITED STATES

Founded 2003 · Pop-culture convention — comics-led

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 4 days, in late February / March. Next edition as officially published: March 5-8, 2026 (announced in RX's 2025 wrap release)

Organizing body: ReedPop, part of RX (RELX) — commercial organizer. Founded independently by Jim Demonakos; acquired by ReedPop in 2015

Organizer leadership: RX Chief Executive: Hugh Jones; Vice President, ReedPop US Comics Portfolio and Star Wars Celebration: Kristina Rogers, quoted as show lead in RX's ECCC releases

Venue: Seattle Convention Center (Summit and Arch buildings)

Scale (as published, with basis): Organizer-reported: over 90,000 fans in 2025 (RX Global release headline, 'Emerald City Comic Con Draws Over 90,000 Fans'); ICv2 reported the 2024 edition was expected at about 85,000 over 4 days. Basis NOT STATED by RX — FLAGGED as ambiguous (presumed tickets / admissions, not certified uniques)

Who exhibits & attends: Comics publishers (Penguin Random House, Mad Cave Studios and others in 2025), 450+ Artist Alley participants (2025, RX), Writers Block literary guests, film and television guests, gaming and cosplay, including the Cosplay Central Crown Championships qualifier

Media / press: Press badges by advance application through ReedPop / ECCC press registration with editorial verification, as published on emeraldcitycomiccon.com

Distinctiveness: The Pacific Northwest's flagship show, comics-led within the ReedPop portfolio, with a major literary Writers Block and a two-building downtown footprint across Seattle's old and new convention centres

Access / entry: Fans: badges sold online through ECCC's ticketing system after a published on-sale; 4-day badges sell out first. Companies: exhibitor, Artist Alley and Writers Block applications through ReedPop

Website: https://www.emeraldcitycomiccon.com · Contact: Via contact and exhibitor enquiry forms on emeraldcitycomiccon.com; RX / ReedPop corporate enquiries on rxglobal.com

RX releases give rounded 'over 90,000 fans' without a counting basis or an exhibitor-audited total; LIKE records the figure as organizer-reported with basis unstated, not as unique attendance

7. C2E2 (CHICAGO COMIC & ENTERTAINMENT EXPO) — CHICAGO, ILLINOIS, UNITED STATES

Founded 2010 · Pop-culture convention — comics and entertainment expo

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 3 days, in spring (April in 2024). Next edition as officially published on c2e2.com

Organizing body: ReedPop, part of RX (RELX) — commercial organizer

Organizer leadership: RX Chief Executive: Hugh Jones; Vice President, ReedPop US Comics Portfolio: Kristina Rogers, quoted as show lead in RX's C2E2 2024 release

Venue: McCormick Place

Scale (as published, with basis): Organizer-reported: over 85,000 fans in 2024 (RX Global, April 2024, for April 26-28, 2024) and a record over 100,000 attendees in 2025 (RX, April 2025, as cited in show records). Basis NOT STATED by RX — FLAGGED as ambiguous

Who exhibits & attends: Comics publishers and creators, nearly 500 Artist Alley participants in 2024 (RX), film and television guests, gaming, literary guests and the Cosplay Central Crown Championships (global cosplay competition hosted at C2E2)

Media / press: Press badges by advance application through ReedPop / C2E2 press registration with editorial verification, as published on c2e2.com

Distinctiveness: The Midwest's ReedPop flagship, launched by the NYCC team in 2010; home final of the Cosplay Central Crown Championships in the years cited and a strong comics-retailer presence for the direct market

Access / entry: Fans: badges sold online through C2E2's ticketing system (3-day and single-day, VIP tiers). Companies: exhibitor and Artist Alley applications through ReedPop

Website: https://www.c2e2.com · Contact: Via contact and exhibitor enquiry forms on c2e2.com; RX / ReedPop corporate enquiries on rxglobal.com

As with ECCC and NYCC, RX's rounded totals carry no published basis. The 2025 'record' claim rests on the organizer's own release headline; no independent count was located

8. DRAGON CON — ATLANTA, GEORGIA, UNITED STATES

Founded 1987 · Multi-genre fan convention — science fiction, fantasy, gaming, comics, literature, art, music, film

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 5 days, over the US Labor Day weekend (Thursday to Monday). Next edition as officially published: September 3-7, 2026 (40th anniversary)

Organizing body: Dragon Con, Inc. — commercial, fan-founded convention company; organized for fans, run with a large volunteer staff (not a nonprofit). Parent has traded as Dragon*Con / Dragon Con, Inc.

Organizer leadership: President: Pat Henry; Vice President: Sherry Henry, as identified in Atlanta Convention & Visitors Bureau coverage of the 2026 40th-anniversary banner reveal ('Pat and Sherry Henry, president and vice president of Dragon Con')

Venue: Five downtown host hotels (Hyatt Regency Atlanta, Atlanta Marriott Marquis, Hilton Atlanta, Westin Peachtree Plaza, Courtland Grand Hotel) plus AmericasMart Buildings 2 and 3

Scale (as published, with basis): About 75,000 attendees in 2025, up from more than 72,000 in 2024, as reported by the Atlanta Convention & Visitors Bureau / Exhibit City News and Dragon Con's own releases. Basis: memberships sold (Dragon Con sells memberships, capped by badge sales in 2025 reporting) — treated as unique members for the edition, not daily turnstile, but the organizer does not publish an audited methodology — see notes

Who exhibits & attends: Some 300 authors, artists, game designers, scientists and experts and major film / television guests across 38+ programming tracks (2025); exhibitors and dealers in AmericasMart, fan tracks run by volunteers; attendees from all 50 states and overseas

Media / press: Press / media badges by advance application through dragoncon.org media registration, with verification and published media policies

Distinctiveness: A city-within-a-city hotel convention and a fan-run counter-model to the commercial expo: 3,500+ hours of programming, the Dragon Con Parade through downtown Atlanta, a major charity programme and blood drive, and the Dragon Awards

Access / entry: Fans: memberships (badges) sold online at tiered prices that rise toward the show, for the full 5 days or single days; host-hotel rooms are allocated separately and are notoriously scarce. Companies: dealer / exhibitor tables in AmericasMart by application through Dragon Con

Website: https://www.dragoncon.org · Contact: Via contact forms on dragoncon.org; media relations through the Dragon Con Media Relations team as published on the site

One Dragon Con-published retrospective in 2026 described 2025 as 'more than 80,000 people' / an '85,000-attendee mega-con' in quoted commentary, against the 75,000 figure in ACVB and post-show reporting — discrepancy flagged; LIKE uses 75,000 (2025) as the dated, sourced figure. Dragon Con is frequently misdescribed as a nonprofit; it is a for-profit company with charitable programmes

9. WONDERCON — ANAHEIM, CALIFORNIA, UNITED STATES

Founded 1987 · Pop-culture convention — comics, film, television

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 3 days, in late March / early April (Easter weekend in 2024: March 29-31, 2024). Next edition as officially published on comic-con.org / wondercon

Organizing body: Comic-Con International (San Diego Comic Convention) — California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation, 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Founded in the San Francisco Bay Area; moved to Anaheim in 2012

Organizer leadership: President of the Board of Directors, Comic-Con International: Robin Donlan; Chief Communications and Strategy Officer: David Glanzer (see San Diego entry for sourcing)

Venue: Anaheim Convention Center

Scale (as published, with basis): Not published. Comic-Con International does not publish an annual attendance figure for WonderCon in the releases checked. Secondary sources routinely cite '60,000+' from pre-2020 reporting (Popverse: 'regularly has 60,000+ attendees') — that is not a dated organizer-published result and is not used as a current figure

Who exhibits & attends: Comics publishers and creators, film and television presentations, Artist Alley and small press, gaming and masquerade; the spring, comics-forward sister show to San Diego Comic-Con

Media / press: Press badges by application and verification through Comic-Con International, on the same credentialing basis as San Diego, as published on comic-con.org

Distinctiveness: CCI's second convention: the accessible, comics-centred alternative to the sold-out San Diego show, in the Anaheim resort district opposite Disneyland

Access / entry: Fans: a free Comic-Con Member ID is required; badges sold online in an announced sale and, unlike San Diego, have historically remained available closer to the show. Companies: exhibitor and Artist Alley applications through CCI

Website: https://www.comic-con.org/wondercon/ · Contact: Via contact forms and Member ID account messaging on comic-con.org

NAMED GAP: no current official attendance. Any WonderCon total in listicles traces to mid-2010s figures (about 60,000 in 2016 is the last widely confirmed number in the sources checked)

10. LOS ANGELES COMIC CON (L.A. COMIC CON) — LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA, UNITED STATES

Founded 2011 · Pop-culture convention — comics, gaming, sci-fi, anime

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 3 days, in autumn (December in 2023; autumn dates in later editions). Next edition as officially published on lacomiccon.com

Organizing body: Comikaze Entertainment, Inc. — independent commercial organizer. Launched as Comikaze Expo, later Stan Lee's Comikaze and Stan Lee's Los Angeles Comic Con, now Los Angeles Comic Con

Organizer leadership: Chief Executive Officer, Comikaze Entertainment: Chris DeMoulin, quoted as CEO in the show's own post-show releases (2023) and company materials

Venue: Los Angeles Convention Center

Scale (as published, with basis): Organizer-reported: about 122,000 fans in 2023 (12th annual edition, December 1-3, 2023, per Comikaze Entertainment's post-show release as published in entertainment press; one account gives 122,300+). Company materials in 2024-2025 claim 'over 126,000 fans annually'. Basis NOT STATED — FLAGGED as ambiguous

Who exhibits & attends: Comics, anime, gaming and collectibles exhibitors, more than 1,000 artists and vendors and a 400+ Artist Alley in 2023 (organizer release), film and television celebrities, indie creators; a deliberately broad, locally rooted Los Angeles fandom audience

Media / press: Press / media passes by advance application through lacomiccon.com with verification, as published by the organizer

Distinctiveness: Los Angeles' independent home-grown pop-culture convention — the commercial, fan-founded alternative in the same building as Anime Expo, with one of the largest Artist Alleys at a US convention by its own count

Access / entry: Fans: badges sold online (multi-day, single-day and premium tiers), positioned by the organizer as accessibly priced. Companies: exhibitor, Artist Alley and sponsorship sales through Comikaze Entertainment

Website: https://www.lacomiccon.com · Contact: Via contact and exhibitor enquiry forms on lacomiccon.com; Comikaze Entertainment corporate information in its public company materials

The 126,000 'annually' figure in crowdfunding / company materials is a standing company claim, not a dated audited result; LIKE headlines the dated 2023 organizer figure (about 122,000) and flags the basis as unstated

Organizing body: Planet Comicon Kansas City — independent commercial organizer; founded and organized by Chris Jackson (show founder), per the convention's published history

Access / entry: Fans: tickets sold online (3-day and single-day; Saturday the premium day). Companies: exhibitor and Artist Alley applications through the organizer

Website: https://planetcomicon.com · Contact: Via contact forms on planetcomicon.com

Attendance rests on spokesperson statements to local media, not a published post-show audit; the pre-show 'expected 60,000' (2025 preview) vs 'over 70,000' (post-show spokesperson) vs 'around 75,000 expected' (economic-impact statement) spread is flagged in the research notes

EUROPE

12. LUCCA COMICS & GAMES — LUCCA, ITALY

Founded 1966 · Comics and games festival — city-wide festival

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 5 days, late October to early November, around All Saints' Day. Edition as officially published: October 29-November 2, 2025 (59th edition)

Organizing body: Lucca Crea S.r.l. — municipally owned company of the Comune di Lucca (publicly owned commercial organizer). Festival founded as the Salone Internazionale dei Comics (first held in Bordighera in 1965, in Lucca from 1966)

Organizer leadership: Director of Lucca Comics & Games and General Director of Lucca Crea: Emanuele Vietina (festival director since 2017, as published by Lucca Crea / the festival). President of Lucca Crea is named inconsistently across years in secondary releases (Francesca Fazzi was named President in 2020-2021 releases; Vietina was described as President of Lucca Crea in 2023 reporting) — see notes; the consistently published operational leadership is Vietina as Director

Venue: Historic centre of Lucca — pavilions, palazzi, churches and squares inside and outside the Renaissance walls (no single convention centre)

Scale (as published, with basis): Tickets sold (organizer-reported, via festival records as cited in reference and press sources): 314,220 tickets sold in 2023 (November 1-5, 2023); record 319,926 tickets sold in 2022; about 275,000 tickets sold in 2024 per post-show reporting. Basis: TICKETS SOLD over 5 days — a turnstile-type total, NOT unique visitors. A person attending 5 days sells / uses 5 daily tickets or a multi-day pass counted in ticket totals as published; unique attendance is not published

Who exhibits & attends: Italian and international comics publishers, manga publishers, games publishers (Wizards of the Coast and Italian games houses), film and streaming presentations, fantasy literature, Artist and self-publishing areas, cosplay; the whole Italian comics and games industry treats Lucca as its annual market and showcase

Media / press: Press accreditation by advance application through Lucca Crea / the festival press office, as published on luccacomicsandgames.com

Distinctiveness: Europe's largest comics festival by tickets sold, staged not in a hall but across a walled Renaissance city — exhibitions in churches and palaces, games on the walls, pavilions in the piazzas; home of the Gran Guinigi awards (successor to the historic Yellow Kid Awards lineage)

Access / entry: Fans: daily and multi-day tickets / passes sold online in advance, with capped daily availability; many city exhibitions are included with the ticket while the city itself remains open. Companies: exhibitor stands and pavilions contracted through Lucca Crea's exhibitor office

Website: https://www.luccacomicsandgames.com · Contact: Via Lucca Crea S.r.l. contact and press-office channels on luccacrea.it and luccacomicsandgames.com

FLAGGED BASIS and common error: '319,000 participants' (2022) in English-language coverage is tickets sold, not 319,000 different people. Lucca's 5-day ticket total, Anime Expo's turnstile and San Diego's capped uniques are three different measures. Leadership title discrepancy (President of Lucca Crea) is flagged rather than resolved by assertion

13. JAPAN EXPO — PARIS (VILLEPINTE), FRANCE

Founded 1999 · Japanese pop-culture festival — manga, anime, music, traditional culture

Calendar / next edition: Annual, 4 days, in early July. Next edition as officially published on japan-expo-paris.com

Organizing body: SEFA Event (Groupe SEFA / JTS Group lineage) — commercial organizer. Founded by Jean-François Dufour, Sandrine Dufour and Thomas Sirdey

Organizer leadership: Leadership not published by name (no president / CEO / show director named) on the official japan-expo-paris.com / SEFA Event materials checked in October 2026. Founders as published in festival histories: Jean-François Dufour, Sandrine Dufour and Thomas Sirdey — do not present the founders as current executives without a current source

Venue: Parc des Expositions de Paris-Nord Villepinte

Scale (as published, with basis): Organizer-reported: 200,000 visitors in 2024 (July 11-14, 2024), down from 255,259 visitors in 2023, per the organizers' post-show figures as reported in French press coverage. Basis: visitors over 4 days (turnstile-type total); unique attendance is not published. The drop was attributed by the organizers to the exceptional summer of 2024 in France (Olympic Games period and the political context)

Who exhibits & attends: 900 exhibitors in 2024 and 2,861 accredited professionals (organizer figures), manga publishers (French manga is a major market), anime studios and streamers, Japanese music acts, video games, traditional-culture pavilion WABI SABI, martial arts, fashion and cosplay; 487 artists and 579 events in 2024 (organizer figures)
