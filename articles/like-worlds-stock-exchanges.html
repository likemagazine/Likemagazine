MARKETS · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S STOCK EXCHANGES

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Markets · October 2026

A stock exchange is where a country's companies meet the world's savings. From the New York Stock Exchange floor to Ho Chi Minh City, this guide maps the 36 exchanges and exchange groups that set the price of equity capital — who runs them, what trades there, and where.

This guide is the equity side of the Markets department. Its companion, The World's Bond Markets (59 venues), covers the bond segments of many of these same operators in full; entries here cross-refer to it for bond segments and do not duplicate them. Every entry carries the same field set: operator and ownership, listing status, chair-level and C-suite leadership with exact titles, listed companies and domestic market capitalisation (dated, on World Federation of Exchanges December 2025 statistics where that basis applies), principal indices, trading hours, currency, what trades there in full, participants as published, published contact route, headquarters and footprint, founding, specialties, website and sources. Where a field could not be verified to that standard, the entry says so plainly.
