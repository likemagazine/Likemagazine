INDUSTRY & INFRASTRUCTURE · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD’S ALUMINUM COMPANIES

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Industry & Infrastructure · October 2026

Aluminium is the metal the modern world is built from twice over: once as the light, corrosion-proof material of aircraft, cars, cans and cables, and again as solid electricity — every tonne of primary metal embodies some 13,000 kilowatt-hours, so the industry's map is really a map of cheap power. World production reached 72.86 million tonnes in 2024 (International Aluminium Institute), roughly 60 per cent of it in China, and this guide profiles the twenty companies that matter most to that map: the integrated giants that run the whole chain from bauxite mine to casthouse, the great smelter companies of the Gulf, Norway, Malaysia and the United States, and the downstream rollers and recyclers whose demand pulls the metal through. For each: who leads it in October 2026, with exact published titles; what it owns; how much metal it makes, with the year and basis stated; what it earned, dated and sourced; and how it is reached — with every field that could not be verified named as a gap rather than filled with a guess.

Three cautions run through the guide, and they are stated rather than buried. First, output figures are not all on one basis — Rio Tinto and South32 report at share, Hydro on controlled entities, Hongqiao by sales volume and capacity — so each cell labels its own basis. Second, three entries are context entries by design: Xinfa and SPIC, Chinese producers of world-ranking scale that publish almost nothing in English, and South32, a diversified miner whose aluminium chain alone would rank it in the world's dozen. Third, Hindalco and its Novelis arm appear as two rows — integrated producer and downstream roller — with the relationship flagged in both entries and their tonnages never added together. Rusal, the largest producer outside China, is verified from its Hong Kong filings and graded honestly where English disclosure runs out.
