WEDDINGS · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S WEDDING APPAREL HOUSES & SALONS

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Weddings · October 2026

A wedding dress is the most considered garment most people ever buy, and the business behind it is a world of its own: Barcelona bridal dynasties, Beirut couture ateliers, a New York salon so famous it has its own television show, and tailors in Rome who have dressed grooms since 1945. This guide — the first of LIKE® Magazine's new Weddings department — maps that world house by house.

The list covers 39 outlets: bridal houses with their own boutiques, bridal houses that sell through stockists (labelled plainly as such), the one bridal chain of true global scale, the landmark multi-brand salons brides cross oceans to visit, and a tight set of menswear houses with genuine wedding businesses. For each: what it sells, its own boutiques worldwide (127 locations individually named), its flagship, its official website and a published contact route. Every entry was verified against the house's own site, official group communications or established trade press; company figures are labelled as company figures, and the gaps — South Asia above all — are named at the end rather than padded over.

BRIDAL HOUSES WITH THEIR OWN BOUTIQUES

1. PRONOVIAS — BARCELONA, SPAIN

Founded 1922 · Bridal house · Luxury to premium; the group's broadest ladder runs from accessible-luxury ready-to-wear up to Atelier Pronovias couture

Sells: Bridal gowns (ready-to-wear and Atelier Pronovias couture line), Pronovias Privée, veils, headpieces and accessories; 'Party Edit' evening and occasion dresses (wedding-party and guest wear)

Own boutiques: Barcelona, Spain; Madrid, Spain; Seville, Spain; Rome, Italy; Milan, Italy; Paris, France; Lyon, France; Marseille, France; Lisbon, Portugal; London, United Kingdom; New York, United States; Los Angeles, United States; Miami, United States; Houston, United States; Mexico City, Mexico; Shanghai, China

Boutiques & footprint: Flagship list per Pronovias Group communications. The group reported 95 Pronovias and Nicole own stores worldwide, and more than 4,000 points of sale in over 100 countries (company figures, 2026; points of sale include stockists)

Flagship: Barcelona (historic El Suizo flagship, Passeig de Gràcia) · Website: pronovias.com · Contact: Store locator and appointment booking via pronovias.com; group HQ El Prat de Llobregat, Barcelona

Origins 1922 as El Suizo lace and silk shop, Barcelona; first prêt-à-porter bridal collection 1964. Group also owns Nicole Milano and distributes Vera Wang Bride under licence (see separate entries). Acquired by Bain Capital-led investors 2023.
