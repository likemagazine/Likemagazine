INVESTING IN THE FUTURE · CHIPS ON SHOULDERS PUTS CHIPS IN POCKETS

Videographer & editorial by Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · 1 June 2026 · Science / Technology · Summit Season

Josh WolfePartner & Co-Founder, Lux Capital Alex WitkoffCEO, Witkoff Group Nathan TsrorEconomic Minister to North America
“Chips on shoulders puts chips in pockets.” — Josh Wolfe, Partner & Co-Founder, Lux Capital (on entrepreneurship)

Investing in Israel’s Future brings together three leaders whose careers sit at the intersection of innovation, real estate, and international economic diplomacy.

Josh Wolfe

Josh Wolfe is a co-founder and partner at Lux Capital, where he backs ambitious ideas in emerging technology and helps turn breakthrough science into real-world companies. He is known for supporting frontier sectors like defense, AI, biotech, robotics, and space-related innovation.

Alex Witkoff

Alex Witkoff is CEO of Witkoff Group, overseeing development and operations. He also serves on the board of the U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum.

Nathan Tsror

Nathan Tsror serves as Economic Minister to North America and heads the Israel Economic Mission to the U.S. East Coast, strengthening trade, investment, and strategic ties between Israel and American partners. He has more than two decades of experience with Israel’s Ministry of Economy and Industry.

Capital, leadership, and public diplomacy

Together, these three figures represent a mix of capital, leadership, and public diplomacy: breakthrough technology investment, real-estate development and scale, and the economic bridge between Israeli innovation and North America.
