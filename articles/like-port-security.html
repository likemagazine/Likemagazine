TRANSPORTATION · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

PORT SECURITY: THE FIRMS THAT GUARD THE GATES

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Transportation · October 2026

"Port security" is not one industry, and a guide that pretended otherwise would mislead. In practice the work divides three ways, and this guide is organised on exactly that division. The specialists — Ambrey, Dryad, ICTS and their peers — sell security as a service: embarked teams, facility guarding, intelligence. The terminal operators — APM Terminals, DP World, PSA, ICTSI — mostly do not buy it at all: they run security in-house, to the ISPS Code, as a department of the terminal. And the screening majors — Smiths Detection, Rapiscan, Leidos — build the machines that see inside the box. A reader who understands those three models understands the field.

One honest structural note: the specialists almost never name their clients, and several publish no revenue at all. Where this guide cannot verify a figure or a name to its standard, the entry says so — the silences are part of the record of an industry whose product is discretion.

The McKay lead. Michael directed this guide to include "McKay, created in Los Angeles" — a port-security company of that name founded in Los Angeles. The research is reported in full in the Method section below. Its conclusion, stated here so no reader misses it: no port-security company named McKay, founded in Los Angeles or elsewhere, could be verified from trade, company or registry sources, and the lead is therefore carried as UNVERIFIED — not written up as an entry, and not quietly dropped either. The documented maritime McKay/Mackay names are three, and none fits: Mackay Communications (marine electronics and satellite services, headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina, with a Los Angeles service office), McKay International Engineers (container-crane engineers of Oakland, merged into Liftech Consultants in 2022), and ISS–McKay (a New Zealand port agency, now wholly Inchcape's). A genuine Los Angeles port-security firm with a near name — McRoberts Maritime Security of San Pedro, in the Port of Los Angeles complex — is included on its own merits, and it is expressly NOT asserted to be the firm Michael meant.

METHOD, GAPS AND EXCLUSIONS

THE McKAY LEAD — FULL REPORT. Searches run October 2026 across web and trade sources for: "McKay" + port security / maritime security / Port of Los Angeles / San Pedro / California security company. Findings: (1) Mackay Communications, Inc. (mackaycomm.com) — marine electronics, navigation and satellite-communications sales and service; founded lineage in marine radio; HQ Raleigh, NC; its published network includes a Los Angeles office (San Pedro area, +1 310 519 7539, la@mackaymarine.com). It is a service company for shipboard electronics — not a security firm, and not Los Angeles-founded.
