HEALTH · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Health · October 2026

Behind every famous hospital name stands an operator: a board, a chief executive, a balance sheet and a workforce the size of a city. This guide — the flagship list of LIKE® Magazine's Health department — maps 40 of the world's major hospital groups and health systems, from Rochester, Minnesota to Bangkok, with the full field set for each: who leads them, who owns them, how large they are, what they do best, whom they serve, and how to reach them.

The roster spans nonprofit academic systems (Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, Johns Hopkins Medicine), America's largest for-profit operator (HCA Healthcare), integrated payer-providers (Kaiser Permanente), Europe's great public flagships (Charité in Berlin, AP-HP in Paris, Karolinska in Stockholm), Singapore's public clusters (SingHealth and the National University Health System), India's listed hospital groups (Apollo, Fortis, Medanta), Southeast Asia's private networks (IHH Healthcare, KPJ Healthcare, Bangkok Dusit Medical Services, Bumrungrad), Australia's Ramsay Health Care and St Vincent's, South Africa's Mediclinic, Netcare and Life Healthcare, Korea's Samsung, Asan and Severance hospitals, Canada's University Health Network, Brazil's Einstein and Rede D'Or, Israel's Sheba Medical Center, and the Aga Khan health system across Asia and Africa. Every entry was verified against official publications in October 2026. Where a field is not published — a group chief financial officer at a public hospital, a consolidated bed count at a federation — the entry says so plainly rather than filling the space. Leadership transitions already announced for 2026 and 2027, at Mayo Clinic, Netcare, Ascension and King's College Hospital among them, are recorded with their dates.

NORTH AMERICA

1. MAYO CLINIC — ROCHESTER, MINNESOTA, UNITED STATES

Founded 1889 · Nonprofit academic medical center and health system

Ownership / parent: Mayo Clinic (independent nonprofit; governed by its Board of Trustees)

Leadership: President and CEO: Gianrico Farrugia, M.D. (in post since Jan. 2019; Mayo announced May 12, 2026 that he completes his tenure Dec. 31, 2026, with a successor to be elected Nov. 2026). CFO, COO and chief medical officer names/titles: not consolidated on a single official page consulted — see mayoclinic.org leadership pages; not stated here rather than guessed.

Facilities and scale: 85,000-person global workforce; destination campuses in Rochester (Minnesota), Phoenix/Scottsdale (Arizona) and Jacksonville (Florida), plus Mayo Clinic Health System community hospitals and clinics across Minnesota, Wisconsin and Iowa, and Mayo Clinic Healthcare in London (Mayo Clinic news releases and Becker's reporting, 2026). 2025 revenue $21.5 billion (company-reported).

Specialties of note: Full-spectrum tertiary and quaternary care; consistently top-ranked in cardiology and heart surgery, oncology, neurology and neurosurgery, gastroenterology, orthopedics, transplant, endocrinology (diabetes), nephrology, pulmonology, geriatrics, psychiatry, and complex/undiagnosed disease programs; Mayo Clinic Platform (AI and data). Patient categories: Patients from all 50 U.S. states and more than 135 countries (Mayo Clinic, 2026); insured, self-pay and international patients; specific payer categories not separately disclosed.

Contact: Official route: appointment request and contact via mayoclinic.org; international patient offices listed on mayoclinic.org. No general published email.

Sources: Mayo Clinic news releases (2026); Becker's Hospital Review (May 12, 2026; May 19, 2026); Mayo Clinic community update, Feb. 2026.
