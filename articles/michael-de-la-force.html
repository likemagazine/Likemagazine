MICHAEL DE LA FORCE

Chairman and Editor in Chief of LIKE Magazine, photojournalist, and actor.

By LIKE Magazine · November 18, 2026

Michael de la Force is an American publisher, photojournalist, and actor. He is Chairman and Editor in Chief of LIKE Magazine and Chief Executive Officer of Leadership Media Development Corporation. He has been a member of SAG-AFTRA since 1998 and is a member of the New York Press Club.

Publishing career

De la Force began his career with Leaders Magazine in 1994, serving as International Projects Manager from 1994 to 2013, and maintains his press credentials with the magazine to date. He has served as Chairman and Editor in Chief of LIKE Magazine since 1994. His work as a publisher and photojournalist has taken him to interviews with heads of state and government, including President Ólafur Ragnar Grímsson of Iceland, President Gerald R. Ford, and Premier Li Peng of China, and to the Great Hall of the People in Beijing with Henry O. Dormann and Jiang Zemin in 1995.

Acting

As an actor, he appeared on the CBS daytime drama As the World Turns from 2000 to 2010, and on All My Children, of The Walt Disney Company and ABC Television, from 1998 to 2008. His film work includes a photographer in Andy Tennant’s Sweet Home Alabama and an FBI agent in Dick Lowry’s The Line of Duty: Ambush in Waco.

Photojournalism

De la Force holds White House press pool credentials. He photographed President Barack Obama in Bridgeport, Connecticut, in 2010. His photographs of public figures, from presidents to cultural icons, form a large part of the LIKE Magazine archive.

Education and recognition

He attended Coweta High School (1992) and Cornell University (2021). He is listed as Duc Michael Shane De La Force in Marquis Who’s Who in America, 2020-2021. In 2008 he received the Presidential Commission of the National Republican Congressional Committee, signed by Chairman Tom Cole on May 2, 2008. He was named a Leading National Blue Ribbon Salesman by the Future Farmers of America, of which he is a lifetime member, and completed New York State Citizen Public Health Training.

Memberships

New York Press Club; SAG-AFTRA; Sons of the American Revolution, by documented lineage; New York Academy of Sciences; The Knights and Chancellor of St. John of Jerusalem; Pan-Pacific and Southeast Asian Women’s Association; Committee of Inclusion, Pacifica Radio. Pacifica Radio is the listener-supported network of the Pacifica Foundation, which owns five stations of its own, KPFA in Berkeley, KPFK in Los Angeles, KPFT in Houston, WBAI in New York, and WPFW in Washington, and supplies programs to more than 200 affiliated stations nationwide.

Faith and service

He is a member of Fifteenth Street Monthly Meeting in New York City. He serves as Co-Clerk of the Indigenous Affairs Committee of New York Yearly Meeting of the Religious Society of Friends, appointed in 2024 for a term through 2027; Julie Finch serves as the committee’s other co-clerk.

Family and lineage

De la Force descends from Joseph Loomis of Windsor, Connecticut, whose homestead, built in 1640, still stands on the campus of the Loomis Chaffee School, never moved from its original ground. His Revolutionary ancestors include Captain Comfort Hoyt and Captain Moses Chamberlin, both documented in the records of the Daughters of the American Revolution. Through the Cherokee missions at Brainerd, his line runs to Chief George Lowrey, Assistant Principal Chief of the Cherokee Nation, whose daughter Lydia Lowrey married Dr. Milo Ard Hoyt at Brainerd in 1820. The full documented descent is set out in LIKE Magazine’s “Roots of a Republic” (June 29, 2026).
