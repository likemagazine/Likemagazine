INDUSTRY & INFRASTRUCTURE · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD’S MINING COMPANIES

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Industry & Infrastructure · October 2026

The modern world is dug before it is built: every railway, battery, turbine and data centre begins in a pit, a shaft or a stope owned, in the main, by the twenty-five companies in this guide. They include the houses that move a third of the world's seaborne iron ore from the Pilbara and Carajás; the copper majors of Chile, Peru, the Congo and Arizona on whom electrification depends; the gold producers of Nevada, Ontario, Ghana and Western Australia; and the coal companies whose tonnages still light half of Asia. This guide maps them all — who runs each company in October 2026 and with what exact title, what it digs and where, how large it is on a dated and sourced figure, and how it may be reached — with every gap named.

Two stories run through the edition. In London and Vancouver, Anglo American and Teck Resources are becoming Anglo Teck — a merger announced in September 2025 that, at press time, awaited only China's antitrust approval, with completion expected by March 2027; both companies therefore appear, accurately, as the separate houses they still are. And in Toronto, Barrick Gold became Barrick Mining in May 2025, changed its chief executive that September, and set about listing its North American gold assets — a reminder that in mining, as in the ground itself, nothing stands still for long.
