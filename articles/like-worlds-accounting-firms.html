MARKETS · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD’S ACCOUNTING FIRMS

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Markets · October 2026

The world's money is counted, audited and advised on by a profession organised in layers: four global networks of a scale all their own; a second tier of networks and associations that follow the middle market across every border; the great national firms of the United States, several of them now reshaped by private equity and by the two largest firm transactions of the decade; and, newest of all, networks built from China to follow Chinese enterprise around the world. This guide maps all four layers — who leads each organisation in October 2026, how its member firms fit together, what it sells, where it stands, and what it publishes about its size — with every figure dated and every gap named.

The list covers 32 organisations: the Big Four; 16 global networks and associations; 10 United States national majors; and 2 China-origin networks. For each: its global leader and the full published executive team, its ownership and member-firm structure, its services in full, its published client categories (no organisation in this guide publishes a client list, and none is invented), its revenue and people with their dates and sources, its headquarters and footprint with named key offices, its official website and its published contact route. Where a field could not be verified it says so in the entry. Moss Adams, Marcum, BKD and Ruihua are covered inside the Baker Tilly, CBIZ, Forvis Mazars and Crowe entries, where their stories now belong.

THE BIG FOUR

1. DELOITTE — LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM

Founded 1845 · Global network (Big Four)

Global leader: Joe Ucuzoglu, Deloitte Global CEO (since 1 January 2023); Sharon Thorne, Chair of the Deloitte Global Board

Global leadership: Global CEO: Joe Ucuzoglu. Chair of the Global Board: Sharon Thorne. Global Chief Operating Officer: Donna Ward (leads the Deloitte Global Operating Committee, per Deloitte's leadership and governance pages). Deloitte does not publish a single global CFO title; finance is led within the global executive structure and the full named global executive roster is published on deloitte.com's leadership pages — service-business leaders are organised under the four businesses below.

Services: Audit & Assurance; Tax & Legal; Strategy, Risk & Transactions; Technology & Transformation (the four businesses in Deloitte's current structure, reorganised from the former Audit, Consulting, Financial Advisory, Risk Advisory and Tax & Legal lines)

Clients: Client categories as published: nearly 90% of the Fortune Global 500 (Deloitte's own figure); individual audit and consulting clients are not named as a list — not disclosed

Revenue: US$74.5 billion aggregate global revenue, FY2026 (year ended 31 May 2026), +3.8% in local currency, announced 24 September 2026 (Deloitte global release) · People: More than 500,000 people (FY2026 release; 470,000+ in FY2025)

Footprint: 150+ countries and territories · Key offices: Global coordinating office in London; largest member firms in New York (Deloitte US), London, and across EMEA and Asia Pacific

Structure: Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu Limited (DTTL), a UK private company limited by guarantee, is the coordinating entity; it provides no client services. Client services are delivered by legally separate DTTL member firms in each country (e.g., Deloitte LLP in the US, Deloitte LLP in the UK, Deloitte Tohmatsu in Japan), which share the brand, standards and methodology.

Website: deloitte.com · Contact: Contact via deloitte.com country and office pages and web forms; London HQ switchboard +44 (0)20 7936 3000 as published. No published general email — Deloitte routes enquiries through its site.

Founded 1845 in London by William Welch Deloitte. Touche Ross merged with Deloitte Haskins & Sells in 1989 to form Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu; the brand shortened to Deloitte in 2003. Deloitte announced a US$3 billion investment through 2030 in AI and delivery modernisation in its FY2026 release.

Sources: Deloitte global revenue announcement, 24 Sep 2026 (deloitte.com); Deloitte leadership and governance pages (deloitte.com); LIKE network survey, Oct 2026
