MARKETS · FIXED INCOME

THE WORLD'S BOND MARKETS: WHO RUNS THEM AND HOW

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Markets · October 2026

A bond is an IOU: governments and companies borrow from investors and pay back with interest. The venues where those IOUs are issued, listed, and traded — from the U.S. Treasury market, the deepest pool of money on earth, to a retail bond board in Warsaw — are the bond markets. They set the price of borrowing for every mortgage, corporate loan, and national deficit. That is why they command the respect James Carville once gave them: "I used to think that if there was reincarnation, I wanted to come back as the president or the pope or as a .400 baseball hitter. But now I would like to come back as the bond market. You can intimidate everybody."

This list covers 59 significant bond markets and fixed-income trading venues across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, Latin America, and Africa — sovereign and corporate bond markets plus the major electronic trading platforms, grouped by region. Every entry gives the venue's official trading-segment names, the operator and its leadership (full C-suite where published), how leaders are selected, how trading works and who may trade, what is traded with scale figures where published, official contact details, and the operator's annual-report cadence. "UNVERIFIED" marks what could not be confirmed — never guessed.

NORTH AMERICA

1. BROKERTEC — U.S. TREASURY INTERDEALER MARKET (CME GROUP)

United States — New York, New York (BrokerTec Americas LLC is headquartered in New York; the new BrokerTec Chicago CLOB aligns cash trading with the Chicago futures market).

Trading as: BrokerTec — the dealer-to-dealer (interdealer) central limit order book (CLOB) for on-the-run benchmark U.S. Treasuries, plus U.S. and EU repo (BrokerTec Stream, BrokerTec Chicago CLOB). Also trades via CME Globex.

Operator: BrokerTec Americas LLC, an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of CME Group Inc. (registered broker-dealer with the SEC and member of FINRA).

Leadership: Terrence A. (Terry) Duffy — Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, CME Group. Lynne Fitzpatrick — President and Chief Financial Officer (becomes CEO in March 2027, when Duffy becomes Executive Chairman). Mike Dennis — Global Head of Fixed Income, CME Group. Matt Gierke — Global Head of BrokerTec.

Hierarchy & selection: Public company (Nasdaq: CME). Board of Directors elected by shareholders; annual meeting typically held in May in Chicago; CEO appointed by the board. In June 2026 CME announced a CEO succession: Terry Duffy steps down as CEO March 2027 (becoming Executive Chairman), Lynne Fitzpatrick becomes CEO. Selection process details beyond the announcement: UNVERIFIED in sources checked.

How it works: Electronic interdealer CLOB (anonymous matching) plus RFQ and streaming protocols, via CME Globex and the BrokerTec API. Institutional/dealer access only (120+ dealer customers trade daily). Trades settle delivery-versus-payment / receipt-versus-payment; BrokerTec holds FICC membership and self-clears U.S. Treasury transactions. Cash leg settlement through FICC; the SEC's December 2023 clearing mandate requires eligible cash Treasury transactions to be centrally cleared from December 31, 2026 (repo from June 30, 2027).

What it trades: Benchmark on-the-run cash U.S. Treasuries and U.S./EU repo. Approximately $700 billion notional in fixed income products traded daily across BrokerTec platforms; Q1 set an all-time single-day average daily notional volume record of $1.05 trillion across the D2D CLOB, D2C RFQ and streaming platforms. New BrokerTec Chicago CLOB (launched October 6) trades all seven on-the-run benchmark Treasuries in smaller notional sizes with tighter increments, aligned to the futures market; "Treasury Link" spread trading between CBOT futures and BrokerTec cash expected Q4 2026 (pending regulatory review).

Oversight: SEC (cash securities), FINRA (broker-dealer reporting), Federal Reserve (primary-dealer supervision; Treasury market structure oversight shared with Treasury/SEC). CME Group exchanges are CFTC-regulated.

Website: https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/brokertec.html (parent: https://www.cmegroup.com)

Investor Relations: +1 312 466 7459, investors@cmegroup.com; Shareholder Relations: +1 312 930 3484, shareholder.relations@cmegroup.com.
