MARKETS · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S INSURANCE BROKERAGES

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Markets · October 2026

Every company of consequence buys its insurance through a broker, and the brokers themselves have become some of the largest professional-services firms on earth. This guide — a Markets department subset of LIKE® Magazine's worldwide guides — maps the 25 firms that lead the industry, ranked by 2025 total revenue in the AM Best Top Global Insurance Brokers ranking, 2026 edition, and refreshed to October 2026.

For each firm the guide gives the full field set wherever verifiable: the full C-suite and chair-level leadership with exact titles; clients or client categories where published; published contact details and the official contact route — published emails and phones appear only where the firm itself publishes them, and are never guessed; specialties and practice areas in full; office locations, headquarters plus footprint; the founding year; ownership, parent and listing; and revenue, dated and sourced. Where a field could not be verified, the entry says so plainly rather than leaving a silent blank.

Three transactions frame the 2026 edition. Marsh McLennan became simply Marsh on January 14, 2026, and reported in October that the brand transition was complete. Aon agreed in August 2026 to acquire USI Insurance Services from KKR for $17 billion in cash; the deal remained pending at this guide's October 22, 2026 refresh, with a close expected in the fourth quarter. And Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. closed its $13.45 billion acquisition of AssuredPartners in August 2025, the largest sale of a U.S. insurance broker to a strategic acquirer in industry history; AssuredPartners keeps its own entry, flagged as a Gallagher subsidiary.
