TECHNOLOGY · THE WORLDWIDE GUIDE

THE WORLD'S AI COMPANIES

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Technology · October 2026

This guide covers 52 AI-first companies and independent frontier labs, drawn from the CB Insights AI 100 (2025 edition) and the Forbes AI 50 (2025 edition) and refreshed to October 22, 2026. Big-technology in-house AI divisions are out of scope, with one deliberate exception: Google DeepMind, which operates as a frontier lab. For every company the guide gives the same full field set: founded; headquarters and office footprint; website and published contact route; founders and full published leadership (chair, chief executive, president, chief financial officer, chief technology officer, chief scientist and board where the company publishes them); ownership and independence status; funding and valuation, dated and sourced; what it builds and sells; specialties; clients or client categories where published; and what makes it distinctive. Where a field could not be verified it says so plainly in the entry — "Not published" or "Not disclosed in sources checked" — rather than being guessed or left silent. Published contact emails appear only where the company itself publishes them: that is five of the 52 companies, which is the industry norm, and no company in the roster publishes a public phone number.

The roster is in motion. Three transactions define the autumn of 2026 and none of them has closed: Nvidia's $12.93 billion acquisition of Hugging Face (definitive agreement signed September 2, 2026; closing expected in the first half of 2027), AMD's $8.2 billion all-stock acquisition of World Labs (announced September 28, 2026; closing expected by the end of 2026), and the Cohere–Aleph Alpha merger (definitive agreement signed September 16, 2026; closing expected before the end of 2026). SpaceX has completed its acquisitions of xAI and of Cursor. Cerebras listed in May 2026, MiniMax and Zhipu AI in Hong Kong in January 2026, and Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 protection on October 12, 2026, with Disney as stalking-horse bidder. Every entry states its company's status as of October 22, 2026.
