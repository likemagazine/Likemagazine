AS TIME GOES BY, TIMELESS ELEGANCE REMAINS: SUMMER’S GUIDE TO STYLE FROM THE SCIONS OF FASHION

By Michael de la Force · Editor-in-Chief, LIKE® Magazine · Style

Summer is the season of effortless glamour—when style icons, both past and present, inspire us to embrace our individuality and exude confidence.

Here, we spotlight the visionaries who have shaped the language of elegance: Chanel Coco, Anna Wintour, Fern Mallis, Madame Lanvin, Ralph Lauren, Halston

and Arnold Scaasi. Each has left an indelible mark on fashion, and their words and work continue to define timeless style.

COCO CHANEL: THE ARCHITECT OF MODERN ELEGANCE

Coco Chanel revolutionized women’s fashion by liberating them from corsets and introducing simplicity, comfort, and understated luxury. Her career began in the early 20th century, and her legacy endures in the iconic Chanel suit, little black dress, and pearls.

“Fashion changes, but style endures.”

“Simplicity is the keynote of all true elegance.”

Chanel’s philosophy: elegance is not about following trends, but about being true to oneself.

ANNA WINTOUR: THE POWERHOUSE OF FASHION JOURNALISM

Anna Wintour, editor-in-chief of Vogue since 1988, is the most influential woman in publishing. She transformed the magazine into a cultural force, championing emerging designers and shaping global trends. Wintour’s leadership has made the Met Gala the most anticipated fashion event of the year.

“You either know fashion or you don’t.”

Wintour believes in the power of conviction and clarity—her style is defined by her signature bob, dark sunglasses, and unwavering confidence.

FERN MALLIS: THE VOICE OF FASHION

Fern Mallis is best known as a creator of New York Fashion Week, bringing structure and global attention to the industry. Her career has spanned decades, and she is celebrated for her sharp wit and no-nonsense approach to style.

“Fashion is about storytelling—your own story, told through your clothes.”

Mallis champions individuality and authenticity, encouraging people to dress for themselves, not for others.

MADAME LANVIN: THE PIONEER OF HAUTE COUTURE

Jeanne Lanvin founded the House of Lanvin, the oldest French fashion house still in operation. Known for her romantic, feminine designs, Lanvin was a visionary who dressed generations of women in timeless elegance.

“Elegance is a kind of harmony—between the inside and the outside.”

Lanvin’s legacy is her ability to blend tradition with innovation, creating garments that are both beautiful and enduring.

RALPH LAUREN: THE ALL-AMERICAN ICON

Ralph Lauren built an empire on the idea of classic American style. From polo shirts to tuxedos, his designs evoke luxury, nostalgia, and effortless cool.

“I don’t design clothes. I design dreams.”

Lauren’s approach to elegance is about aspiration and storytelling—creating a world where anyone can feel sophisticated and at home.

HALSTON: THE MASTER OF SIMPLICITY

Roy Halston Frowick, known simply as Halston, redefined American fashion in the 1970s with his minimalist, fluid designs. He dressed icons like Liza Minnelli and Jackie Kennedy Onassis.

“You’re only as good as the people you dress.”

Halston believed in the power of simplicity—letting the fabric and the wearer’s personality shine.

ARNOLD SCAAASI: THE KING OF GLAMOUR

Arnold Scaasi was known for his bold, theatrical designs and celebrity clientele. He dressed everyone from Barbra Streisand to first ladies.

“I like clothes that make a statement—but not a loud one.”

Scaasi’s elegance was about confidence and joy, proving that glamour and sophistication can coexist.

SUMMER STYLE TIPS FROM THE ICONS

• Chanel: Embrace simplicity—less is more.

• Wintour: Wear what makes you feel powerful.

• Mallis: Tell your story through your wardrobe.

• Lanvin: Find harmony in your look.

• Lauren: Dream big and dress accordingly.

• Halston: Let your personality shine through simplicity.

• Scaasi: Don’t be afraid to make a statement—with confidence.

This summer, channel the timeless wisdom of these style legends. Elegance isn’t about fleeting trends—it’s about confidence, authenticity, and a touch of daring. Let their words and work inspire your own signature look.
