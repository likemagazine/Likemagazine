## Coverage

11 HQ countries: France, Italy, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States, Japan, India, China, Germany, Brazil, Australia. CEOs verified for 32 of 52 maisons; the rest are family-run without a public CEO title or the name could not be verified — left blank rather than guessed.

## Interpretation notes

- Boutique rows come from official maison store locators and cover **maison-owned boutiques**. Department-store concessions, wholesale doors, and authorized retailers are excluded.
- Counts may include shop-in-shops where the maison lists them as boutiques (e.g., Damiani's large count reflects its wide Italian network).
- Mauboussin's count includes the "diffuseurs" (authorized retailer partners) listed on its official boutique finder, since the maison presents them as points of sale; they are identifiable by the "BIJOUTERIE" naming in `boutique_name`.
- Fashion houses appear only for their high-jewelry operations (Chanel, Dior, Hermès, Louis Vuitton); their boutique counts reflect high-jewelry salons, not fashion stores.
- JAR (Paris) and Bhagat (Mumbai) have no public website by design; source_url is blank with an explanatory note.
