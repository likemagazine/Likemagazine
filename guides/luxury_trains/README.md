# World's Luxury Trains

A sourced list of the world's notable luxury trains and luxury rail cruises.

- **Compiled:** 2026-10-20
- **Total trains:** 42
- **File:** `trains.csv` (columns: `name, operator, route, countries, region, note, source_url`; sorted by region, then name)
- **Every row carries a verified official operator/booking website URL.**

## Method

Built by four parallel regional researchers using official operator websites first,
cross-checked against Condé Nast Traveler / Travel + Leisure luxury-train roundups and
reputable rail press. Duplicate removed at merge time (Eastern & Oriental Express appeared
in both the Europe and Asia drafts; the Asia entry was kept). No blank fields.

## Interpretation notes

- **Gran Tenku** (Nankai Electric Railway) — added October 2026: daytime premium
  sightseeing train launched April 24, 2026 (Osaka Namba–Gokurakubashi, the Mount
  Koya gateway, ~90 minutes); 4 cars, ~70 passengers, seasonal onboard dining,
  two daily round trips. Included on the same basis as Bernina Express and
  The Royal Express (dedicated premium daytime sightseeing experiences).
- **Nusantara Explorer** (PT Kereta Api Indonesia) — added October 2026:
  Indonesia's first luxury sleeper, launched September 29, 2026; 12-car, 36 berths,
  Grand Restaurant and lounge. Operates on whole-train charter and scheduled
  open-trip models with on-request Java itineraries — no published routes or
  commercial details as of October 2026 (official KAI launch press page verified;
  no dedicated product page found on kai.id). Included as a genuinely launched
  luxury product with scheduled departures planned; charter-only Royal Canadian
  Pacific remains excluded as a private-charter service with no regular departures.
- **La Dolce Vita Orient Express** — new 5-day/4-night Rome–Istanbul international
  itinerary launched October 22, 2026 (inaugural; return Istanbul–Rome October 30),
  from €20,000pp; the train's first journey beyond Italy (via Venice, Budapest,
  Brașov, Sinaia); reflected in the route and countries fields.

- **Glacier Express (Excellence Class)** and **Bernina Express** are included on their
  premium/luxury tiers; the base services are premium scenic trains rather than luxury sleepers.
- **The Canadian (Prestige class)** is a regular VIA Rail long-distance service included
  only on the basis of its Prestige-class luxury offering.
- **Grand Canyon Railway** and **Napa Valley Wine Train** are day-excursion luxury
  experiences rather than overnight sleeper journeys.
- **The Blue Jasmine** (DTH Travel / State Railway of Thailand) — added October 2026:
  Thailand's new heritage luxury sleeper (launched November 16, 2025): restored
  1960s Japanese carriages with Jim Thompson interiors, 37 guests, 9-day Bangkok–
  Chiang Mai round trip (Ayutthaya, Uthai Thani, Sukhothai) with 2 nights onboard
  plus heritage hotel nights; scheduled departures booked through 2026 and into
  2027 (official booking page verified). Included on the same basis as other
  rail-cruise journeys that pair nights onboard with hotel nights.
- **Dream of the Desert** (Saudi Arabia) — note corrected October 2026: inaugural
  season moved from December 2026 to **September 2027**, per a September 2026
  interview with the train's general manager at the Arabian Travel Market; 2–3
  night desert journeys with AlUla links announced.
- **Golden Eagle Silk Road Express** — launched September 2026 (inaugural China
  departures sold out); Beijing–Tashkent Grand Silk Road journey.
- **Golden Eagle Vietnam Express** — launched April 2026 (Treasures of the East
  departure Ho Chi Minh City–Shanghai).
- **Canyon Spirit** (Armstrong Collective) — new luxury train brand launched April 2026:
  the Rocky Mountaineer US operation (Denver–Moab since 2021) rebranded, with the
  new 3-day Denver–Salt Lake City Rockies to the Red Rocks Extension. The Canadian
  Rocky Mountaineer routes keep their name.
- **Venice Simplon-Orient-Express** — new Paris–Amalfi Coast "Villeggiatura by Train"
  itinerary debuted May 4, 2026; reflected in the route field.
- **Golden Eagle Danube Express** — new 14-day "Aurelian Odyssey" Paris–Cappadocia
  itinerary (Istanbul, Izmir/Ephesus, Denizli, Ankara) debuted September 12–25, 2026,
  priced from €28,000pp; reflected in the note field.
- **The Vietage** is a luxury railcar attached to scheduled services, not a standalone train.
- **Venice Simplon-Orient-Express** — new one-night Paris → Monte-Carlo journey
  announced October 2026 (Belmond press release, Oct 5): Paris Gare de l'Est –
  Monaco-Monte-Carlo via Cannes (~19h35), first departure May 10, 2027, to
  become an annual fixture; continues to Verona and Venice. Corroborated by
  Travel Tomorrow, La Revue des Hôtels, International Traveller. Reflected in
  the route and countries fields (Monaco added).

## Known gaps / exclusions

- **Tren a las Nubes (Argentina)** — active heritage/tourist train, but not luxury.
- **Shongololo Express** — brand absorbed into Rovos Rail (announced 2022); not a standalone service.
- **Premier Classe (South Africa / PRASA)** — long-distance services suspended since October 2024;
  current operation could not be verified.
- **Royal Rajasthan on Wheels** — no verifiable evidence of current operation.
- **Royal Canadian Pacific** — luxury but primarily private-charter, no verified regular departures.
- **Belmond Grand Hibernian** — ceased operation October 2019; carriages relaunched as the
  Britannic Explorer (included).
- **American Orient Express / GrandLuxe** — defunct.
- **Inca Rail** — premium but no genuinely luxury tier verified.
- **Alaska Railroad GoldStar** — premium dome service, not a luxury train.
- **Jordan Hijaz steam rides** — real and operating, but budget tourist excursions (~20 JD), not luxury.
- **Royal Railway Cambodia** — basic economy service, not luxury.
- **Trenitalia Espresso Riviera** — heritage-style sleeper (Italy–France), suspended
  for the 2026 season; luxury tier not verified, so not listed.
- **Portugal ultra-luxury train project** — government plan targets first journeys
  ~2030; development phase scheduled September 2026 – August 2029 (per Portuguese
  government project plan, reported in TheStreet, August 2026). No operator/train
  name verified, not listed. Watch item for future checks.
- **North Africa (Morocco, Egypt)** — no genuine luxury rail service found; Egypt's sleepers are
  standard first-class.
  - Watch: Egypt's **Guardian of the Nile**, a luxury "rail cruise" to the Nile Valley marketed
    for service from 2027 (reported in National Geographic, September 2026); not yet
    bookable or verified against an operator site.
  - Watch: Uzbekistan's **Samarkand Express**, planned to connect four Silk Road cities
    (reported in National Geographic, September 2026); no operator or launch date verified.
  - Watch: Norway's **Norient Express** (SJ Norge), a six-day electric Bergen–
    Trondheim via Oslo luxury package (gym car, observatory, boutique hotel stays).
    Repeatedly announced (Oct 2025 target, then 2026) but no official booking
    page or confirmed actual departure verified as of October 2026; not listed
    until a launch can be verified.
  - Watch: China's **Jinxiu Tianfu (锦绣天府)** "Splendid Sichuan" trainset, part of the
    Chengdu Panda Express family — reported as an 18-car ultra-luxury sleeper with
    Song-dynasty/Shu-brocade interiors, passenger rooms and suites, and 24-hour
    "Panda butler" service, running 13–16-day "travel by night, sightsee by day"
    Chengdu→Urumqi/Xinjiang rail cruises (Tianchi, Kanas, Sayram Lake, Nalati,
    Kuqa, Kashgar, Turpan). 2027 departures (May–October) and prices (from
    ~RMB 67,000–99,000pp) are published by third-party luxury-train and travel
    outlets (chinaexpresses.com, inboundcntour.com, travelandtourworld, Oct 2026
    reporting; sources disagree on whether 2027 is the 2nd or 3rd year of
    operation), but no official operator or booking page could be verified as of
    October 2026 — bookings appear to run through Xinjiang Pathfinder
    International Travel Service. Watch item: verify against an official source
    before listing.
