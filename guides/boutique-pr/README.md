# Boutique PR & Communications Firms — Worldwide Guide

October 19–20, 2026. 199 independent boutique PR and communications firms across 61 countries, organized by specialty.

## Boutique criteria
1. **Independent ownership** — not owned by WPP, Omnicom, Publicis, Interpublic, Havas, Dentsu, or a PE-backed rollup; founder/partner-owned.
2. **Small team** — roughly 2–50 people.
3. **Small footprint** — 1–3 offices in a single country.
4. **Niche flavor** — a named specialization rather than generalist scale.

Anchor example: Belfer Communications (belfercom.com), Monmouth County NJ — independent, founder-led boutique specializing in luxury/fine living, arts & entertainment, finance (SEC/FINRA-compliant social), nonprofit and beauty & wellness.

## Verification discipline
Every field comes from the firm's official website. `contact_email` holds only published addresses; `founders`/`team`/`key_people` hold only names on official pages; `client_roster` holds only publicly disclosed clients; `media_placements` only what the firm publicizes about its own work. Blank or `UNVERIFIED` where a fact could not be confirmed — never guessed.
