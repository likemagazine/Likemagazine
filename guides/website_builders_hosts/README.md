# The World's Website Builders & Hosts — LIKE® Magazine, New Technology Department

**Guide date:** October 2026 · **Roster:** 35 companies — Part A, Builders & Platforms: 16 · Part B, Hosts & Infrastructure (including the three hyperscale majors): 19

## Field set (applied to every entry, no exceptions)

For every company, wherever verifiable: (1) full leadership with exact titles — CEO, chair, CFO, CTO and equivalents; (2) what it sells in full — builder, hosting types, domains, e-commerce, email and AI tools as actually offered; (3) customers and scale — dated, sourced figures where officially published, customer categories otherwise, "not disclosed" where neither exists; (4) pricing posture in words only (entry / mid / premium — no price figures); (5) published contact details — official contact route, and published emails/phones only where the company itself publishes them, never guessed; (6) headquarters plus footprint — data-centre regions or office locations as published; (7) founded; (8) ownership, parent and listing. Any field that could not be verified says so plainly in its cell; the column stays in the schema for every entry.

## Structure and honesty rules

- Two parts, one roster. Part A is builders and platforms; Part B is hosts and infrastructure.
- **Dual entries:** GoDaddy and Hostinger each appear once, covering both builder and host businesses, and are labelled DUAL ENTRY. They are not double-counted.
- **Majors context:** Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud are framed honestly as hyperscale clouds, not shared hosts. Cloudflare and DigitalOcean are likewise described as what they are (connectivity cloud; developer cloud), not as conventional shared hosts.
- Leadership was checked for October 2026 currency. Notable 2025–26 changes carried in the entries: Hostinger appointed Giedrius Zakaitis CEO on May 26, 2026; OVHcloud reunited chairman and CEO under founder Octave Klaba on October 20, 2025; Automattic confirmed Matt Mullenweg's return as chairman and CEO in September 2026 after a two-day interim under CFO Mark Davies; Webflow has been led by Linda Tong since June 2024; Squarespace has been private under Permira since October 2024, with Anthony Casalena continuing as CEO and chairman.
- Sources: company releases, results and corporate pages consulted in October 2026; where a figure comes from company reporting it is labelled as such in the cell.
