## Methodology and rules

- Official published sources only: operator websites, press releases, annual reports, regulatory filings, and trade press (SpaceNews, Via Satellite) for merger/status verification only.
- **Blank beats guessed.** Where no official phone, email, CEO, or fleet figure could be verified, the field is left blank or marked "not published"/"undisclosed", and the gap is documented in `notes`.
- Fleet figures count in-service satellites only, each tagged with its source year; ordered/future spacecraft are excluded and noted separately.
- Classified military programs are marked undisclosed — never speculated.

## Systematic gaps (honest limitations)

1. **Phones and general emails are sparse industry-wide.** Satellite operators and space agencies route contact through web forms or press contacts; 12/58 phones and 9/58 emails are published, the rest blank with notes.
2. **Constellation sizes are not officially published for Starlink, Amazon Leo, Umbra, or HawkEye 360.** Independent-tracker figures (e.g. ~11,100 Starlink working satellites, Sept 2026) are recorded in `notes` only, never in `fleet_size`.
3. **EchoStar publishes no consolidated in-service fleet count** — marked "not published".
4. **CNSA's civil fleet is marked "undisclosed"** (civil/military control split across CNSA/PLA structures); Roscosmos given as "~364–400 spacecraft total orbital group (Sept 2026)" with military detail undisclosed.
5. **EUMETSAT's fleet total (12) was derived from official programme pages** — no consolidated official figure found; NSIL-owned/transferred comm-sat count likewise has no official single figure.
6. **Lanteris Space Systems is a manufacturer, not a fleet operator** — retained as a single row to document Maxar's post-split structure; www.lanterisspace.com is the company-stated official domain (verified 13 Oct 2026) but currently redirects to parent Intuitive Machines' corporate site — no standalone Lanteris-branded site; flagged in `notes`.
7. One leadership transition is mid-flight (CSA) and flagged in `notes`; CONAE's vacancy was filled 26 Aug 2026 (Josefina Pérès).

## Exclusions

- **UK Space Agency** — funds/administers UK civil missions but operates no satellite fleet; absorbed into DSIT in 2026 while keeping its name.
- Absorbed brands kept as single combined rows, not separate entries: Intelsat (→ SES), Inmarsat (→ Viasat), OneWeb (→ Eutelsat Group), Yahsat/Bayanat (→ Space42), Lynk Global/Omnispace (→ Elveo Mobile), Maxar Technologies (→ Vantor + Lanteris).
- Telesat Lightspeed, Eutelsat OneWeb, and SES mPOWER are covered within their parent rows, not duplicated.
- Pure launch providers with no operated fleet, ground-segment-only companies, and resellers are out of scope.
