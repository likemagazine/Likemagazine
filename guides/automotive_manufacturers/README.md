## Methodology

- **Roster:** groups (not brands) ranked by full-year 2025 global sales/production volume. Brands are enumerated per group in the `brands` column.
- **Ranking backbone:** Focus2Move World Car Group Ranking (full-year 2025, registrations in 159 countries) for ranks 1–10 — Toyota 1, Volkswagen Group 2, Hyundai-Kia 3, Renault Nissan Alliance 4, Stellantis 5, GM 6, Ford 7, Geely 8, BYD 9, Honda 10. Ranks 11–30 cross-checked against company-reported 2025 sales. Ranks ~29–~43 are **estimated** against published 2025 group volumes and labeled with `~`: OICA discontinued its manufacturer tables after 2017 and Focus2Move publishes a top 30 only, so no published source ranks groups 31–50 for 2025.
- **Methodology note:** Focus2Move (registrations-based) and OICA-style sold-vehicle tallies order some groups differently (e.g. BYD 6th vs 9th, SAIC 7th). The per-row `sales_rank` records which basis applies.
- **Sales vintage:** labeled per row in `notes` — most groups calendar 2025; Japanese groups reporting on fiscal years (Honda, Suzuki, Subaru, Mitsubishi: FYE March 2026) and Isuzu (FYE March 2026) are flagged as such; Tesla reports deliveries.
- **Leadership:** full names + exact titles as published by each manufacturer.
- **Model timelines (2016–2026):** official press archives first; where official archives are incomplete, reputable automotive press (Autocar, Automotive News, Reuters, CnEVPost for Chinese EV makers) fills gaps — the source tier is labeled per row in `sources`, and press-sourced dates are never presented as official. Caps (~15 added / ~15 discontinued for large groups, ~10 for tail groups) keep the column to significant nameplates.
- **Markets:** `markets_not_sold_or_restricted` lists ONLY verifiable cases with a stated reason (sanctions, regulatory, strategic withdrawal). Nothing is inferred.
- **Contacts:** emails and HQ phones appear only where the manufacturer publishes them; most do not.

## Known gaps and exclusions

- The tail (ranks ~29–~43) carries thinner disclosure by nature: leadership titles for Iran Khodro/Saipa flagged "as last published" (both in ownership flux — IKCO privatized 2025, Saipa IPO process started Oct 2025); Isuzu's figure is fiscal-year; Sinotruk has a two-source volume discrepancy, flagged in-row.
- **Excluded from the top-50 tail after verification:** Neta (bankruptcy restructuring, ~zero 2025 volume), VinFast (196,919 deliveries), Rivian (42,247), Lucid (15,841), PACCAR (144,200 trucks), Volvo Group trucks (202,911), Iveco (~130–140k), Proton (157,976) — all below the ~300k top-50 cutoff. Ultra-luxury marques (Ferrari et al.) do not reach top-50 volumes.
- Commercial-heavy groups (Daimler Truck, Traton, Sinotruk, Isuzu) are included with truck/bus share noted; Traton's 25% Sinotruk stake is cross-noted to avoid double-count confusion.
- Premium figures mix 2024/2025 vintages where noted; sales figures are not directly comparable across fiscal/calendar bases — each row states its basis.
