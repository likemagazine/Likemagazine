## Methodology and rules

- Civil aviation focus. Official published sources only: manufacturer websites, press releases, corporate reports, and published order books.
- **Blank beats guessed.** Aviation OEMs are overwhelmingly contact-form-only; where no official HQ phone or general email exists, the field is blank and the gap is documented in `notes`.
- Dual-use manufacturers are included with military lines noted (e.g., Dassault's Rafale, Boeing Defense, Sikorsky's Black Hawk heritage, Bell's military programs).
- `notable_clients` uses publicly disclosed orders only. Airliner OEMs publish order books, so that segment is well populated; business-jet and GA makers systematically disclose no individual customers, and those rows say so honestly.

## Systematic gaps (honest limitations)

1. **HQ switchboards and general emails are largely unpublished.** Only 10 of 38 publish an HQ phone and 4 a general email (mostly the small makers — Konner, Dynali, Composite-FX — plus source-labeled media/sales contacts for a few majors). The majors (Airbus, Boeing, Gulfstream, COMAC, ATR, Textron, Dassault) route everything through web forms.
2. **Business-jet and general-aviation customer identities** are systematically unpublished by every OEM in this set.
3. **No Middle East, Oceania, or Africa airframe OEMs** at this inclusion tier.
4. **Founding years** are blank for 4 rows where no official founding date is published (Curti Aerospace, Cicaré, Dynali, Composite-FX).

## Exclusions

- Pure military manufacturers (no separate civil output).
- Dead programs and dormant makers: Mitsubishi SpaceJet (program terminated Feb 2023, Mitsubishi Aircraft Corp. dissolved), Mooney (M20 line closed since 2019/2020; parts/support only), RotorWay (ceased 2021; successor's Chapter 11 dismissed 2023–24).
- Absorbed brands carried under parent rows: Viking Air inside De Havilland (consolidated Feb 2022, amalgamated Aug 2024), Pipistrel inside Textron Aviation (eAviation reporting division ended Jan 2026).
- Pure eVTOL startups (Joby, Archer, Lilium) — excluded by scope; would require widening the inclusion tier.
