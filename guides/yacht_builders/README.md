## Methodology and rules

- Official published sources only: yard websites, official press releases, and reputable trade press (Boat International, Superyacht Times, Yachting press) where official material was unavailable.
- **Blank beats guessed.** Where no official phone, email, CEO, or owner could be verified, the field is left blank and the gap is documented in `notes`.
- Every row has a `history` field: a concise founding story/milestones drawn from official materials, except where no official history exists (noted in `notes`).
- Owner names appear only where the yard discloses them or reputable press reports them — never speculated.

## Systematic gaps (honest limitations)

1. **Yards are contact-form-only by industry norm.** Only 37 of 75 publish an HQ switchboard and 26 a general email; the rest route contact through web forms or named sales staff. Every blank is explained in `notes`.
2. **Named owners are rare.** Most yards disclose hulls, not owners; the notable-yachts field carries famous delivered yachts, with owner names only where publicly disclosed.
3. **Key people are thin for private family yards.** Several rows rely on trade press for leadership; flagged per-row in `notes`.
4. **Region skew is geographic reality.** 62 of 75 yards are European; luxury yacht building is concentrated in the Netherlands, Germany, and Italy. Oceania (2), Asia (2), Africa (1), Middle East (2), North America (6) reflect where the luxury tier actually builds.
