# The World's Accounting Firms

**A LIKE® Markets Guide** — the worldwide accounting-firms guide of the Markets department (the professions grouping) of LIKE® Magazine (likeleaders.com).
Compiled October 2026. Staged only — not published.

## Scope and roster (32 entries, in four sections)

1. **The Big Four** — Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG.
2. **Global Networks & Associations** (16) — BDO, RSM, Forvis Mazars, Grant Thornton, Baker Tilly, Crowe, PKF, Moore Global, HLB, Nexia, Kreston Global, PrimeGlobal, Allinial Global, UHY, ETL Global, Russell Bedford.
3. **United States National Majors** (10) — CLA (CliftonLarsonAllen), Plante Moran, CohnReznick, EisnerAmper, CBIZ, Aprio, Cherry Bekaert, Wipfli, Citrin Cooperman, Withum.
4. **China-Origin Networks** (2) — SW (ShineWing), Reanda International.

Networks are treated at the global level, with member-firm structure explained in every entry. National majors appear only where they are genuinely independent firms or networks in their own right — not as Big Four member firms (Deloitte Tohmatsu, PwC Zhong Tian and their peers are covered inside their networks' entries, not listed separately).

## Field set (applied to every entry, in CSV, PDF/DOCX and web article alike)

Name; guide section; type / structure class; headquarters city and country; founded; global leader (chair/CEO, with exact title and start date where published); **global leadership — the full published C-suite / executive team** (global CEO, chair, CFO/COO equivalents and service-line leaders where the organisation publishes them); ownership and member-firm structure; services and specialties in full; clients or client categories where published, else "not disclosed"; global revenue and people numbers, dated and sourced from official global reviews or filings; headquarters plus office footprint (published office/country counts, named key offices); official website; published contact details (official contact route; published emails/phones only where the organisation itself publishes them — never guessed); notes; sources. **Any unverifiable field says so plainly in the cell; the column stays in the schema for every entry.**

## Coverage (of 32 entries)

- Headquarters city and country: 32/32. Founded: 32/32 (with origins labelled where a modern firm dates from a merger).
- Named global leader (chair/CEO): 32/32 — including the 2025–26 successions: KPMG (Gary Wingrove, from 1 Oct 2026), Grant Thornton International (Greg Keith, from 1 Jan 2026), BDO (Pat Kramer to Nov 2026; Trond-Morten Lindberg incoming), Moore Global (Vivienne Muir, Jul 2026), Allinial Global (Tony Sacre, Mar 2025), Nexia (Matthew Howell, Jan 2025).
- Full published executive team named beyond the top leader: strongest for EY (complete Global Executive with service-line vice chairs), Plante Moran (nine group managing partners), Deloitte, PwC, CLA, Aprio and CohnReznick (dual-CEO structure). Most networks publish no global CFO/COO title; each such entry says so plainly rather than inventing one.
- Revenue carried with a date and source: 27/32. People carried: 27/32.
- Published general emails: 2/32 (Baker Tilly International, ETL Global) plus member-firm emails published by SW Hong Kong and Reanda Hong Kong — the profession routes contact through directories and web forms, and the guide prints the route rather than guessing an address.
- Clients named as a list: 0/32 by design — no organisation in the guide publishes a client list; every entry carries its published client categories or "not disclosed".

## Named gaps — stated honestly

- **Nexia, UHY, HLB and SW revenue:** no official global-review figure was verified for this edition; the cells say so. LIKE's earlier network survey recorded member-material figures for some of these; they are not carried as headline numbers.
- **Plante Moran revenue:** the firm describes itself as a billion-dollar firm and publishes no precise current-year figure in sources consulted.
- **Cherry Bekaert 2026 revenue:** its rise to No. 17 in Accounting Today's 2026 Top 100 was announced without the revenue figure in the release consulted; the dated 2025 figure (US$660 million) is carried.
- **EY FY2026 revenue:** trade coverage of Value Realized 2026 reported US$57 billion; the official FY2025 figure (US$53.2 billion) is carried in the table pending the full official release in sources consulted. Both are stated in the entry.
- **RSM revenue bases:** the network's calendar-2024 US$10 billion announcement and the US$9.973 billion figure recorded in LIKE's survey are carried side by side with their dates, not merged.
- **UHY chairmanship:** recorded as not verified (no official source found), following the standing survey.
- **CBIZ officers beyond the CEO:** CBIZ is listed, so its officers are a matter of SEC filings; only the CEO is named here, with readers directed to the latest proxy statement.
- **By design, excluded:** Big Four national member firms as separate rows; firms below the national-major scale in their home markets; and associations beyond PrimeGlobal and Allinial Global (AGN, BKR, DFK, ECOVIS, GGI, IAPA, IECnet, Integra, MGI, MSI, Morison and Praxity appear in LIKE's separate network survey and, for Praxity, inside the Plante Moran entry).
