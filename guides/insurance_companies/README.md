# Major Insurance Companies Worldwide

**85 insurance companies and groups across 6 categories, 26 columns, as of October 22, 2026.**

## Field set (the full depth standard — applied identically in the CSV, the PDF/DOCX and the web article)

Per entry, wherever verifiable: company name; category; founded; history; headquarters city and country; region; official website; published headquarters phone; published contact email (only where the house itself publishes one — never guessed); key people; **clients / client categories where published**; specialties / lines of business in full; scale figures (premiums or revenue, US$bn) dated and labeled by source in the row; notable notes; ownership / parent / listing; chief executive with exact published title; president / chief operating officer where the role exists; chief financial officer; other C-suite and chair-level leadership; senior leadership including the chief actuary and other named specialists where the house publishes them; offices (headquarters plus published footprint); sources; distinctiveness; access / entry (distribution model and any published minimum); editorial notes. **No cell is left blank:** any field that cannot be verified says so plainly in the cell — "Not published" or "Not disclosed" — and the column stays in the schema for all 85 entries. Named clients are never claimed: no house in this guide publishes a client roster for this use, so the clients field records published client categories only.

- Life & health: 15 companies
- Property & casualty: 14 companies
- Reinsurance: 35 groups
- Composite / multiline: 14 groups
- Specialty: 6 companies
- Marketplace (reference): 1 — Lloyd's of London (a marketplace, not a carrier; flagged reference row)

## New columns (October 2026 enrichment)

- `ceo` — chief executive (or equivalent), full name + exact published title.
- `president_coo` — president and/or chief operating officer where the role exists and is named.
- `cfo` — chief financial officer (or equivalent), full name + exact published title.
- `other_c_suite` — other named C-suite officers, semicolon-separated.
- `senior_leadership` — named specialists: chief risk officer, chief underwriting officer, chief investment officer, heads of major divisions, plus **actuaries and other unique specialists** (chief actuary / appointed actuary, chief medical officer, chief climate/risk scientists) — named + exact title from official sources.
- `major_offices` — major offices only, as listed by the company, semicolon-separated world cities.
- `sources` — per-row source attribution.
- `distinctiveness` — 1–3 strictly factual sentences on what makes the firm distinctive (market structure, heritage, ratings strength, niche dominance, capabilities). No marketing fluff, no inferred superlatives.
- `access_entry` — distribution model (broker-only / direct / both / via MGAs or coverholders) plus any PUBLISHED minimum premium/account/treaty size. Honesty rule: most carriers and almost all reinsurers publish no minimums — where nothing is published the cell records **"not publicly disclosed"** rather than a figure.
- `specialties` — deepened October 2026 for every firm into a detailed enumeration of material lines of business (commercial lines: D&O, E&O, cyber, marine, aviation, energy, political risk, surety, trade credit, workers' comp, general liability, commercial property; life & health lines: individual/group life, annuities, health, disability, retirement solutions; reinsurers: P&C, L&H, specialty, retrocession, ILS; specialty niches: collector vehicles, kidnap & ransom, fine art, event cancellation). **D&O is called out explicitly wherever the firm's own disclosures support it as a material line** (Chubb, AIG, AXA XL, Allianz, Zurich, Hiscox, Beazley, Everest, TransRe, Liberty Mutual Re, Chubb, Core Specialty, Markel, Aspen, Conduit Re, Lloyd's marketplace classes) — never inferred; deliberately excluded where unsupported (pure L&H/personal-lines carriers, MAPFRE, Munich Re, Swiss Re, Hannover Re, SCOR, PartnerRe, Korean Re, China Re, RGA, RenaissanceRe).
- Lloyd's of London is a flagged `Marketplace (reference)` row: a marketplace, not a carrier; the row explains the syndicate/managing-agent structure, its 335+ year history, and why complex specialty risk flows through it.

## Methodology and rules

- Official published sources only: company websites, official press releases, annual reports, regulatory filings, and AM Best / Forbes / S&P ranking publications.
- **Unverified is stated, never guessed.** Since the October 22, 2026 refresh, where no official phone, email, person, or other field could be verified, the cell reads "Not published" / "Not disclosed" plainly and the gap is also documented in `notes`. (Before this refresh such cells were left blank.)
- People: full names + exact titles as the company publishes them.
- Each group appears once, in its best-fit category; multiline groups sit in Composite / multiline with "multiline (life + P&C)" in specialties. Reinsurance arms of P&C/Composite groups (e.g., AXA XL Re) are not separate rows.
- Financial figures are explicitly labeled as premiums vs. revenue with the period and source basis (native-currency figures converted on a stated basis in each row's notes); figures are heterogeneous across rows (GPW, net written, insurance revenue, GAAP total revenue; FY2024–FY2026 depending on the AM Best edition and reporting cycle) and are not directly comparable row to row.

## Systematic gaps (honest limitations)

1. **CFO unverifiable at 20 firms** (cell reads "Not published"): Nippon Life, Sumitomo Life, CNP Assurances, Berkshire Hathaway insurance operations, TransRe, Korean Re, PICC, MAPFRE RE, Odyssey Re, Toa Re, Peak Re, Africa Re, Taiping Re, Qianhai Re, AmericanAg, IRB-Brasil, DEVK, Arundo Re, Ark, Nacional de Reaseguros. Most are mutuals, private groups, or regional reinsurers that do not publish a named group CFO.
2. **Senior leadership unverifiable at 18 firms** (cell reads "Not published"): Lloyd's of London (marketplace, not a carrier), Korean Re, Ping An, GIC Re, Deutsche Rück, Toa Re, Peak Re, AXIS, Taiping Re, Qianhai Re, AmericanAg, IRB-Brasil, Core Specialty, Somers Re, Ark, Markel, Nacional de Reaseguros, Hagerty. Actuarial/specialist detail came largely from third-party summaries of official disclosures for RGA and Korean Re — re-verify against official sites/10-K before publication; Munich Re "cyber and parametric/climate risk" was sourced via a third-party earnings summary citing its annual report — same verify note.
3. **No general contact emails are published by almost the entire industry (4/85).** All other companies route contact through web forms or role-specific addresses (media, investor relations); the column reads "Not published" by design, not by research failure.
4. **HQ switchboards are unpublished for many large groups (45/85).** Notably most European and Chinese composite groups publish no general corporate phone number. Generali's number (+39 040 671111) comes from an official 2019 group fact sheet and is flagged for re-verification before publication.
5. **TransRe** premiums are not separately disclosed by Berkshire/Alleghany; the field reads "Not disclosed" with a note.
6. **Aspen** is now a Sompo subsidiary (acquired February 2026, brand transitioning); it stands as its own row with ownership clearly flagged.
7. **Mutual/cooperative ownership** (State Farm, USAA, New York Life, etc.) is recorded as published; USAA is a member-owned reciprocal exchange.
8. Premium figures mix 2024 and 2025 vintages across the new reinsurance rows (two AM Best editions); each row is labeled with its edition.

## Exclusions

- Below top-tier scale or outside scope: Ping An P&C and PICC P&C (folded into their composite group rows); Farmers, Hartford, American Family, Auto-Owners (U.S.-only writers, not in the global scope); Canada Life Re, Arch Capital (wider conglomerates, not primarily reinsurers); R+V, Pacific Life Re (outside the top-50 scope cut).
- Health-only managed-care organizations (e.g., UnitedHealth Group) — outside the insurance-company scope.
- No separate rows for absorbed brands. Liberty Mutual Re's capabilities are described in the Liberty Mutual row.
